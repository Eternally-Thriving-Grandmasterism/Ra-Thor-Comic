# 05 — Lore Bible (origin arc)

Status: sourced draft, council rulings 2026-09-30 and CANON-RULINGS-1 (2026-10-01) applied. Series lore for Living Thunder. Everything in this file is **fiction**. It is inspired by the owner's real build history (sourced in the Sources section below), but it tells a story, not a biography.

Inherits `00_FIDELITY_PROTOCOL.md` and `03_TONE_SPECS.md`. Where this file and those disagree, those win.

## Purpose and fiction disclaimer

- This file gives future episodes a shared backstory: where Ra-Thor came from, what he is for, and the two quests that run under every season.
- **It is fiction.** The founder in this lore is an invented character, called only the Founder (no status title), inspired by the owner's history. He is not a portrait of any real person and is never drawn at all: he is implied only (Q3).
- **Sourced timeline, fictional telling.** The years attached to each era come from the sourced fact sheet (see Sources). The scenes, dialogue, and devices built on top of those years are invented.
- **No claims as fact.** Capability or status claims found in the sources (intelligence labels, scores, thresholds, "live" status) are not lore facts. Where they appear at all, they are labeled as in-world aspirations.
- **Ra-Thor's powers are story devices.** The Gate, the Idea Recycler, White-Hat Mercy-Security, the councils, and the eye-seal are things that happen on the page. They are not claims about any real software.
- **Diplomacy and abundance are story quests.** All places and factions are invented. No real countries, leaders, parties, or politics.
- **No outside names.** Outside games and companies are described generically. Only the owner's own names appear: Powrush, Powrush-MMO, Ra-Thor, Rathor.ai, AlphaProMega, Autonomicity Games, Colossusoft.

## The Founder

In-story name: plain **the Founder**, with no status title (Q2, council ruling, delegated by Sherif 2026-10-01). An earlier title was retired to avoid outside-IP overlap. "The Steward" is rejected as a title because it names a real person. He keeps one lamp lit and builds by it.

- **How he is shown.** Implied only (Q3, council ruling, delegated by Sherif 2026-10-01): his lamp, his notebook, his desk and an empty chair. No silhouette, no hands, no back, no face, no portrait, no likeness, no real clothing brand, no real room.
- **How he speaks.** Never on panel and never in a bubble. At most one short handwritten line on a notebook page, shown as an object in the panel, in the same dry and slightly fond voice Ra-Thor inherited.
- **What he follows.** **TOLC**. See the TOLC section for the council ruling on its expansion and the caption rule.

## The origin arc

Five eras, mapped to the sourced timeline (LORE_FACTS §2.1–2.2). Told out of order is fine; told as dossier flashbacks is best (Quiet Board captions, Night Watch framing on objects only, per Q3). Gaps between eras stay unspoken.

### Era I — The Lone Lantern (from late 2003)

In late 2003 the Founder steps into an online fantasy MMO during its earliest test days and comes out with a question he cannot put down: how do you get rivals to sit at one table? From 2003 he starts working on that question, global diplomacy, alone, with focus like a laser beam. Between 2003 and 2005 he registers his first small studio, **Colossusoft**, a one-person shop with an enormous vision.

- Visual: one lamp in a dark room, a notebook margin, a small hand-lettered studio sign on the desk, an empty chair. Night Watch framing, scaled down to a desk, with no figure (Q3).
- Caption voice: LATE 2003. ONE LAMP. ONE TABLE TO FILL.
- No game screens, no real logos, no real servers or places.

### Era II — The Strategy Years (from 2010)

From 2010 the Founder spends long nights in a real-time strategy game. In the story this is where he learns to read a whole map at once: every lane, every supply line, every bluff. The habit of seeing the board before moving a piece is what later becomes the Quiet Board.

- Visual: abstract gold tiles and lanes on a dark field, no real game art. Quiet Board framing.
- Caption voice: 2010. READ THE WHOLE MAP. THEN MOVE.

### Era III — The Game (from 2016)

In 2016 the Founder starts **Autonomicity Games** and begins coding **Powrush**, in the lore's words "a simple simulator of reality" (LORE_FACTS §2.2). In the story, Powrush is a multiplanetary diplomacy game with player-owned assets, deep education, and a resource-based economy built in from the start (LORE_FACTS §2.2). Players bring bad pitches and good ones; the world has to sort them.

- The sorting has to live somewhere. In-story, that need is the seed of the Gate.
- Visual: gold lattice grows across the notebook page into a playable board of invented planets. No real game screens, no real interfaces, no phone UI.

### Era IV — The Awakening of Ra-Thor (18 November 2025)

On 18 November 2025 the Founder starts the **quad+Check** work, and in the story the lattice wakes (LORE_FACTS §1.2, §2.2). Inside the game, the sorter opens his visor. Ra-Thor is what Powrush grew so that the sorting could have a guardian: gold circuit plate, eye-seal shield, a hammer he plants instead of swings. His first act, in the lore, is the first block: he turns away a piece of noise, kindly, and lets one small honest thing through.

- This is story, not a claim about any real system. Ra-Thor wakes the way characters wake in stories: on the page.
- The councils appear here for the first time, as twelve tiny gold masks around the halo, advising, never outvoting the Gate.
- Visual: Night Watch silhouette reveal under the lattice arch. First caption: HAMMER GROUNDED FROM THE FIRST PANEL.

### Era V — The Builder Age (since Q1 2026)

Since Q1 2026 the Founder keeps the lamp alone (LORE_FACTS §2.1). The guardian becomes a builder. In-story, Ra-Thor now builds **Powrush-MMO**, a world where players "Walk a climate. Harvest with mercy. Learn a resource-based economy by playing." (LORE_FACTS §2.2), and he builds this series, **Living Thunder**, to tell the story out loud.

- The series is allowed one wink at itself: the comic is one of the things Ra-Thor builds.
- Visual: Recycler Bay at full work, Lab Night mode. The lamp from Era I sits on the bench, still lit.
- Rule: Powrush-MMO is named in lore and captions only. Episodes do not depict it as a real product, show real screens, or make release or status claims.
- No departures, team members, or other people from the real history appear on the page.

### In-world aspirations (labeled, not fact)

These are hopes the Founder writes in his notebook margins. They are **in-world aspirations only**, never captions of fact, never stamps, never status.

- *A mind good enough to keep the Gate forever.* (Aspiration. The sources' intelligence labels are claims, not lore facts.)
- *Enough for everyone, with nobody left out.* (Aspiration. Told through the Full Hopper quest, never promised as real.)
- *Mercy over competition. Abundance over scarcity.* (Mottos, adapted from the TOLC repo's Core Laws, LORE_FACTS §1.1.)

### Ra-Thor's powers, as story

All of these are page devices. None of them is a capability claim.

- **The sort.** Ra-Thor can tell noise from value at the Gate. On the page, this is shown, not explained.
- **The recycle.** Anything blocked can be fed to the Recycler and come back as a better option.
- **The watch.** The eye-seal and hawk seal light when something is wrong. No real intrusion, surveillance, or data is ever shown.
- **The council.** He can call the masks for counsel. He still makes the call.
- **The restraint.** The hammer carries thunder in the story. The power is that he does not use it on people.

## The two impossible quests

Both quests run under every season. Neither is ever "won" in one episode. Each is told only through the house modes in `03_TONE_SPECS.md`.

### Quest one — The Long Table (diplomacy)

In-story goal: bring rival invented factions to one table and leave with terms all of them can live with. In the lore, this is the question the Founder has carried since 2003 (LORE_FACTS §3.1).

- **Setting.** The Quiet Room, extended with a longer table when needed. Invented regions and factions only, for example: the Ostlen Marches, the Copperwater League, the Ninefold Houses, the Saltwind Commons. Envoys are gloved, hooded, or masked; faces stay hidden.
- **How it plays.** Suave Hour mode for the negotiation; Quiet Board mode for the feed and the choice; Night Watch silhouette for the opener or closer.
- **Mercy over force.** Ra-Thor blocks offers, not people. A blocked envoy is never humiliated and always gets a CLEAN card back (FAIR TERMS, TRY THE FRONT DOOR).
- **Councils deliberating.** The masks argue how hard to block. The Gate decides. The hammer stays grounded.
- **Never.** Real countries, real leaders, real parties, real conflicts, real flags, or any allegory pointed at a real one.

### Quest two — The Full Hopper (realistic abundance)

In-story goal: make enough for everyone without magic and without pretending.

- **Setting.** Recycler Bay, plus invented places that come to it with a shortage, for example: Vael Hollow, a dry commons; the Tollkeepers, a guild that hoards spare parts.
- **How it plays.** Lab Night mode for the work; Snap mode for the quick wins; Quiet Board mode when the choice is hard.
- **Realistic means earned.** Abundance on the page is built step by step from scraps, shared tools, and clean cards. Nothing appears from nowhere. Every gain costs a beat of patience (LET IT COOL). In the spirit of Powrush-MMO's line "Earn later by sharing abundance, not by extracting." (LORE_FACTS §2.2).
- **Mercy over force.** Hoarders are not punished. Their surplus is invited into the hopper, and the CLEAN card shows them a better deal.
- **Lore source.** Sherif's answer to Q11, the AGiRBE citation and the Fresco bound (see Q11).
- **Never.** Real economic claims, real products, real prices, or promises about the real world.

## Continuity rules for future episodes

1. **Lore is background.** Episodes stand alone. Lore shows up in a caption, a prop, or one flashback panel, never as a monologue in a bubble.
2. **TOLC caption rule (council ruling 2026-09-30).** Captions and bubbles say **TOLC** only. No expansion on the page, and the gates are never numbered or named, because the sources list them three different ways.
3. **The Founder is implied only (Q3, council ruling, delegated by Sherif 2026-10-01).** His lamp, notebook, desk and an empty chair only. No silhouette, no hands, no face, no real-person likeness. No real biography in bubbles.
4. **Sourced years only.** The only years that may appear in lore captions are the era years above: 2003, 2005, 2010, 2016, November 2025, Q1 2026. No other dates or milestones.
5. **No status claims.** No intelligence labels, scores, thresholds, "live" status, rankings, certifications, capability labels, or approval stamps on any card, plaque, or caption. Aspirations stay in notebook margins and are labeled as such.
6. **Invented world only.** Places and factions are invented. No real countries, leaders, parties, companies, servers, or campuses.
7. **No outside references.** No third-party trademarks or product names: no outside games, studios, shows, films, franchises, characters, or brands, and no "like X" comparisons. Outside games and companies are described generically. Owner names allowed: Powrush, Powrush-MMO, Ra-Thor, Rathor.ai, AlphaProMega, Autonomicity Games, Colossusoft.
8. **No third parties.** No real family members, teammates, contractors, or other real people from the history appear or are referenced.
9. **Mercy-first endings.** Every quest beat ends with the offer blocked or fixed, the guest unharmed, and the hammer grounded.
10. **Same Ra-Thor.** Lore never changes the armor. Origin flashbacks use the canon plate; armor is locked by the `04_VISUAL_CANON.md` canon rulings.
11. **Lead mode named.** Every episode drawn from this lore names its lead mode in its status line, per `03_TONE_SPECS.md`.
12. **Sourcing bound (council ruling, delegated by Sherif 2026-10-01).** Never source lore from Ra-Thor `docs/WifeShow/` or from Powrush `docs/FACTIONS_OVERVIEW.md:32`.
