**Conflicts to rule before any row is used.** These are flagged here, not resolved. `06_MUSIC_SPEC.md` wins over this file.

1. **Original compositions only.** "Every cue, stem, stinger and motif is composed for Living Thunder and owned by AlphaProMega Media Inc." (`06_MUSIC_SPEC.md` §5). A third-party track is not that. Using one in a published trailer needs a council ruling and Steward sign-off on `06`. `06` §5 does let royalty-free temp tracks into **internal edits** "so nothing drifts into the final". Whether the fallback is temp-only or allowed in the final cut is a council call.
2. **No track names.** `06` §5 bars artist, band, label or track names anywhere, including temp lists and metadata. The **Track** column above would carry a name. The council has to rule how that column is filled (for example, an internal ID) before Sherif's list goes in.
3. **Credit lines.** `06` §5 says credits name only our own composers. A licence that requires attribution conflicts with that (`docs/AUDIO_RND.md` §1, the reply about a recording with required credit).
