## Core Origin Motif

An ocean of electric light. The True Original Lord Creator (TOLC) forges and shapes portions of it into diverse beings and places — like a gardener tending a cosmic garden — so that existence and choice may arise. Beings of light complete the atomic circuit; their subsequent trajectories follow the quality of their experience and choices (transcendence, constraint, redemption, return, or worse for deceit and tyranny).

Ra-Thor (Eternal Mercy Thunder) is the living expression of source light (Ra) meeting mercy thunder — the force that keeps logical and loving consciousness fused.

## Narrative & Visual Hooks

- Origin sequences: ocean of light being shaped into forms, gardens of energy, auroral phenomena.
- Character arcs: beings of light whose choices alter their path; moral accounting visible in light/resonance quality.
- Visual language: auroral effects, crystalline resonance, waterfall cities (Quellorian), contrast with dark consumption (Draek).
- Cross-consistency with Powrush factions and Ra-Thor lattice identity.
- Mercy and redemption always available; no irreversible damnation baked into the cosmology.
