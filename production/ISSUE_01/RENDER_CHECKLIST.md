## 1. How to judge a render

**Step 1, fidelity gate (pass or fail).** Every box in `ISSUE_01_PRODUCTION.md` §3 must tick for every figure and object in frame, and so must every guard:

- [ ] Portrait, 2:3 or taller, at least 1600 × 2560 px (cover: the art window above the title band, at least 300 ppi at final size).
- [ ] Helm: closed angular great-helm with a crest ridge and a T-visor, and compact, angular, up-swept gold wings no wider than the shoulders.
- [ ] Eyes: two glowing yellow-gold eyes behind the T-visor; light only, no face or skin; never green, dark or missing; a single slit is a reject.
- [ ] A slim, empty, glowing gold halo ring behind the head: no eye, diamond, finial, gem or point in or on it, no spokes, no wings on it. No large wings on his back.
- [ ] Intricate black-and-gold circuit-mosaic plate on the chest and shoulders; torso fully plated; not plain black.
- [ ] Chest emblem: a plain round compact gold sun disc with straight, thin, sharp, glowing rays of light radiating across the chest plate, no bolt over it, set into the plate and not hidden by the shield. Not an eye, no bolt on the chest, no rings or medallion, no ornate sunburst, no leaf-shaped, petal-shaped, tapered-blade or wavy rays, no resemblance to any existing hero's chest emblem, no green. No other chest seal.
- [ ] Shoulder seal: a lapis-blue and gold winged Eye of Horus, flat on the pauldron, matching the shield; not emerald; no bird.
- [ ] Shield: a huge riveted tower shield from the shoulder to mid-shin with a pointed base; deep lapis-blue face with gold circuit lines and a riveted gold rim; its only emblem a large gold winged Eye of Horus (with wings) at its centre. Not a kite or round shield, not cut off at the hip, not covering the chest disc; not a gold or emerald face, not an emerald eye.
- [ ] Hammer: polished gold; cross-shaped stepped head about the size of the helm, tiered blocks either side of a raised square centre boss; a glowing digital gold Eye of Horus on the boss; long engraved gold haft with rune knotwork; glowing gold crystal base spike. Not a plain rectangular block. Story pages: head-down, gold sparks at contact. Cover: raised, golden-yellow lightning running into the rune knotwork.
- [ ] Belt buckle, where the waist is in frame: a symmetrical gold winged sun disc with a small rose-gold bolt at its centre; no text, no other mark, not the Autonomicity Games Inc. logo.
- [ ] Proportions: about 8.5 heads tall; nothing stretched or squashed to fit the frame.
- [ ] Lightning, wherever it appears, is golden-yellow: never blue, never green.
- [ ] No emerald anywhere. Lapis blue only on the shield face and the shoulder seal. The eyes, the shield and shoulder-seal eyes, the hammer eye and the sun disc are gold; the buckle's small bolt is rose-gold/copper, the only rose-gold/copper on the figure. No green anywhere: not in sparks, lightning or lights.
- [ ] Glow only on the eyes, the halo ring, the chest sun disc and its rays, the shield's gold eye, the hammer's digital eye, the sparks, the hammer's crystal spike, golden-yellow lightning and the shoulder seal's gold eye.
- [ ] Nothing from the Rejects in either reference (§0).

One miss is a reject and a rerun (`00_FIDELITY_PROTOCOL.md` rule 7). Do not score a render that fails the gate.

**Step 2, quality rubric (1–5 on each line).**

| Line | 5 | 4 | 3 or lower (rerun) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 Canon read | Great-helm, crest, compact helm wings and T-visor, both glowing eyes, the slim empty halo ring, circuit-mosaic plate, the plain rayed sun disc, the lapis-blue tower shield with its gold winged eye, the matching lapis-blue and gold shoulder seal, the winged sun-disc buckle, the hammer's cross-shaped head and digital gold eye read at a glance at print size | All read; one needs a second look | Any canon detail unclear at print size |
| Q2 Staging | Does exactly what the prompt's E01 or `PACING` line says, nothing more | Matches, with a small extra that adds no story (a stray spark) | Adds an object, figure, event or text the prompt does not have |
| Q3 Ink and palette | Bold black ink and dense cross-hatching as in the style post; gold and obsidian, lapis blue on the shield and shoulder seal only; dramatic rim light; glow only on the eyes, halo ring, chest sun disc and rays, shield eye, hammer eye, sparks, hammer spike, golden-yellow lightning and shoulder-seal eye; no emerald | Right style; hatching a little light, glow a little heavy, or palette slightly off, fixable in the colour pass | Painted or rendered modelling on a panel (`04` Q13), sparse hatching, glow spread across the figure, rainbow clutter, any green, or blue anywhere but the shield face and the shoulder seal |
| Q4 Lettering room | Clean space where §7 and `LETTERING_SPEC.md` put the text; keep-clear zones open | Space exists but bubble must shrink or shift | No room without covering a keep-clear zone |
| Q5 Clean image | No garbled letters, watermark, logo, extra limbs, warped lattice or melted plate | One small artifact, fixable by hand | Artifact in a canon detail or anywhere it shows at print size |

Q5 "logo" means any logo or brand mark. The buckle's winged sun disc with its small rose-gold bolt is required, not a Q5 fault; text on the buckle, a logo on it, or a garbled buckle that shows at print size, is (`ISSUE_01_PRODUCTION.md` §6 Style lock).

**Pass bar: every line scores 4 or 5** (a 4–5/5 render), and the gate is passed. Any 3 or lower is a rerun. Q4 does not apply to the cover art (the title goes in the planned bottom band) or to panels with no text, so score those N/A.

**Reference images.** If the tool takes them, give the rathor.ai hero image or the first frame of the Rathor.ai knight video (hammer head-down); R4 is a fallback. Give a raised-hammer frame only for a raised beat; issue #1 has none. The references' gold shield face, wingless shield eye, square hammer head, thick winged halo and missing shoulder seal do not override the prompt, and neither does v17's undersized shield. The style post is a style reference only: its Ra-Thor is not the design. Never give R3 (diamond gem, walker bot) or R5 (out-of-canon blue variant) (`ISSUE_01_PRODUCTION.md` §5.1, §6 Style lock, §6 panel 5.2).

**Cover.** The cover is painted (`04` Q13), so score Q3 on its palette, the guards and the council finish (velvet matte grain, no airbrush gradients, gold sheen only on the type, ornaments, halo and hammer), not on hatching, until D14 is ruled. Compare it with `art/cover_v17_reference.jpg` for frame, title band, lettering and symbols, and fix v17's known misses: the full tower shield, shoulder to mid-shin, with the legs above the title band.
