## 1. How to judge a render

**Step 1, fidelity gate (pass or fail).** Every box in `ISSUE_01_PRODUCTION.md` §3 must tick for every figure and object in frame, and so must every guard:

- [ ] Portrait, 2:3 or taller, at least 1600 × 2560 px.
- [ ] Helm: closed angular great-helm with a crest ridge and a T-visor.
- [ ] Eyes: two glowing yellow-gold eyes behind the T-visor; light only, no face or skin; never green, dark or missing; a single slit is a reject.
- [ ] A large, thick, segmented, circuit-etched gold halo ring behind the head, a clean, unbroken circle, no radial spokes, with big feathered gold wings on its sides. No diamond, finial, gem or point on the halo.
- [ ] Intricate black-and-gold circuit-mosaic plate on the chest and shoulders; torso fully plated; not plain black.
- [ ] Chest emblem: a compact gold sun disc with a thin outer ring and straight, thin, sharp, glowing rays of light radiating from behind and under the bolt, crossed diagonally by the app-icon bolt (bold, wide, Z-shaped, bevelled rose-gold/copper outline, fine circuit traces and nodes in a black interior), medium-small, set into the plate. Not an eye, not a bolt alone, not a plain disc, not a slim, plain gold, flat or rounded bolt, no large or ornate sunburst, no leaf-shaped, petal-shaped, tapered-blade or wavy rays, not a centred oversized bolt on a plain field, not a bolt inside a plain, flat-colour circle, no resemblance to any existing hero's chest emblem, no green. No other chest seal.
- [ ] Shoulder seal: an emerald winged Eye of Horus, flat on the pauldron; no bird.
- [ ] Shield face gold with a gold rim; its only emblem an emerald winged Eye of Horus (with wings).
- [ ] Hammer: polished gold; big stepped square block head with a gold Eye of Horus on its front face; long thin engraved gold haft; glowing pointed gold base spike. Issue #1: head-down, gold sparks at contact.
- [ ] Belt buckle, where the waist is in frame: small, subtle, plain gold; no letters, logo or symbol drawn on it.
- [ ] Lightning, wherever it appears, is golden-yellow: never blue, never green.
- [ ] Emerald only on the two winged Eye of Horus motifs. The eyes, the hammer eye and the sun disc are gold; the chest bolt is rose-gold/copper with a black interior, the only rose-gold/copper on the figure. No other green: not in sparks, lightning or lights.
- [ ] Glow only on the eyes, the chest emblem, the sparks, the hammer's base spike, golden-yellow lightning and the emerald seals.
- [ ] Nothing from the Rejects in either reference (§0).

One miss is a reject and a rerun (`00_FIDELITY_PROTOCOL.md` rule 7). Do not score a render that fails the gate.

**Step 2, quality rubric (1–5 on each line).**

| Line | 5 | 4 | 3 or lower (rerun) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 Canon read | Great-helm, crest and T-visor, both glowing eyes, winged halo ring, circuit-mosaic plate, rayed sun disc and app-icon chest bolt, both winged Eye of Horus motifs and the hammer's gold eye read at a glance at print size | All read; one needs a second look | Any canon detail unclear at print size |
| Q2 Staging | Does exactly what the prompt's E01 or `PACING` line says, nothing more | Matches, with a small extra that adds no story (a stray spark) | Adds an object, figure, event or text the prompt does not have |
| Q3 Ink and palette | Bold black ink and dense cross-hatching as in the style post; gold and obsidian; dramatic rim light; glow only on the eyes, chest emblem, sparks, hammer spike, golden-yellow lightning and emerald seals; emerald only where the guards allow | Right style; hatching a little light, glow a little heavy, or palette slightly off, fixable in the colour pass | Painted or rendered modelling on a panel (`04` Q13), sparse hatching, glow spread across the figure, rainbow clutter, or extra green |
| Q4 Lettering room | Clean space where §7 and `LETTERING_SPEC.md` put the text; keep-clear zones open | Space exists but bubble must shrink or shift | No room without covering a keep-clear zone |
| Q5 Clean image | No garbled letters, watermark, logo, extra limbs, warped lattice or melted plate | One small artifact, fixable by hand | Artifact in a canon detail or anywhere it shows at print size |

Q5 "logo" means a logo the tool drew. The Autonomicity Games Inc. buckle logo placed in finishing from Sherif's file is not a Q5 fault (`ISSUE_01_PRODUCTION.md` §6 Style lock; D24).

**Pass bar: every line scores 4 or 5** (a 4–5/5 render), and the gate is passed. Any 3 or lower is a rerun. Q4 does not apply to the cover art (title goes in a planned band) or to panels with no text, so score those N/A.

**Reference images.** If the tool takes them, give the rathor.ai hero image or the first frame of the Rathor.ai knight video (hammer head-down); R4 is a fallback. Give a raised-hammer frame only for a raised beat; issue #1 has none. The references' wingless shield eye and missing shoulder seal do not override the prompt. The style post is a style reference only: its Ra-Thor is not the design. Never give R3 (diamond gem, walker bot) or R5 (out-of-canon blue variant) (`ISSUE_01_PRODUCTION.md` §5.1, §6 Style lock, §6 panel 5.2).

**Cover.** The cover is painted (`04` Q13), so score Q3 on its palette and the guards, not on hatching, until D14 is ruled.
