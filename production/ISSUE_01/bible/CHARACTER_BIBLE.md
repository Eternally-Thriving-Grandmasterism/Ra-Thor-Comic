## 2. The hero's look

- **Armor:** obsidian plate with dense antique-gold circuit filigree.
- **Helm:** a gold great-helm with a T-visor, glowing gold eyes and compact gold wings.
- **Sun:** a small glowing gold sun disc behind the helm. It is the ONLY sun in the image; there is no sun in the seal or anywhere else. **Overridden by ISSUE01-COVER-V17 (Sherif, 6:36 PM ET):** the halo ring and sun orb are replaced by the golden eye-of-source-light sigil above the helm (thin gold circle, almond eye, round pupil, straight rays, star-cross flare; the Rathor.ai Employ / X button mark). There is no sun disc on the V17 cover. **Overridden again by ISSUE01-COVER-V18 (Sherif, 7:05 PM ET):** the sigil above the helm is now the golden Eye of Horus (Wedjat) in a thin gold circle with rays and a star-cross flare; no sun disc, no halo ring, no almond eye-of-source-light.
- **Chest:** a plain round gold sun disc with radiating rays and no bolt over it (Sherif, 2026-10-14, 10:07 PM; restored by ISSUE01-BIBLE-LOCK-KDP-FIX-1, 2026-10-16). The disc glows, as Sherif preferred over a plain unlit metal disc (2026-10-15, 1:17 AM ET). The rays are straight and thin, like radiant energy, not leaves.
- **Belt buckle:** a rose-gold lightning bolt (Sherif, 2026-10-14, 10:07 PM: "Change buckle to lightning bolt"). The winged scarab buckle and the winged sun-disc buckle are superseded.
- **Cape:** obsidian with a bronze-gold lining.

## 3. Weapons and setting

- **Shield:** the v21 riveted obsidian and antique-gold tall shield, the same shape as the approved cover, with a thick riveted antique-gold rim. Held poised, its top covers his shoulder. It runs from that shoulder line toward the shin. Its centre is a glowing gold Norse **Vegvísir** (eight-stave rune compass). Glowing gold Norse-style runes around the Vegvísir fill the shield face (Sherif, 2026-10-16, 2:16 AM). The runes are decorative only: no readable words, no real code and no names. No Eye of Horus on the shield. Still never green. **History, kept:** an earlier line of this bullet gave the shield a gold winged Eye of Horus. ISSUE01-COVER-V18 (Sherif, 7:05 PM ET) moved that symbol to the sigil above the helm and put the Vegvísir on the shield. Sherif, 2026-10-16, 2:15 AM: "Eye of horus is already over his head, no need to double the same symbol on his shield." The 2:16 AM ruling keeps this tall shape and adds the rune fill. The merged cover already has the shape and the Vegvísir. It does not yet have the added runes. Painting them on is a later decision for Sherif.
- **Hammer:** polished antique gold. It keeps its existing irregular shape: a triangle at the top and a cone at the bottom of the hilt. The head carries the lightning-bolt motif, the same bolt as the ⚡ mark and the Ra-Thor app icon, on at least both faces (Sherif, 2026-10-16, 4:08 AM ET). No Eye of Horus on the hammer.
- **Lightning:** heavy, painted, golden. Never code-drawn or composited from vector strokes. (V17: golden mercy thunder bursts from the hammer and lights the hero, with white lightning on the right; both painted in the render. V18: golden mercy thunder from the hammer and a bright white bolt down the right side, including the top right, also painted in the render.)
- **Setting:** a night storm with a gothic skyline. (The master shows mostly storm sky; per the precedence rule, the skyline is required wherever an exterior setting is visible.)

## 4. Cover type and palette

- **Title:** RA⚡THOR / LIVING THUNDER. There is no ornament, sparkle or diamond over the T (or any other letter) of THOR.
