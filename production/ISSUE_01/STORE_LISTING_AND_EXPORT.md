## 3. Kindle export checklist (from the §1 pages)

**Files and page size**

- [ ] One JPEG per page, text already on the image (Kindle Create has no text tools). Name them in order: `ra-thor-01-0001.jpg` … (cover first, then title page, story pages 1–8, credits).
- [ ] Every page the same pixel size, so pages 4–5 can be set as facing pages.
- [ ] KDP states its 1920 × 1200 ratio in landscape terms. For portrait pages, read it as 1200 × 1920 (width × height, 1:1.6). That reading is ours, not KDP's wording; confirm in the Previewer.
- [ ] Crop each bleed page (6.875 × 10.5 in) to 6.5625 × 10.5 in, centred, to reach 1:1.6. This trims 0.156 in from each side of the bleed and about 0.03 in of trim; the 0.25 in safe area is untouched (`ISSUE_01_PRODUCTION.md` §10).
- [ ] Export at 2400 × 3840 px (KDP's 200% "for small text" class, portrait). The tiny bubble in 6.1 needs it (`LETTERING_SPEC.md` §3). 1800 × 2880 (150%) is the floor.
- [ ] JPEG, sRGB, at least 300 ppi. Not CMYK.

**Kindle Create**

- [ ] New project, Comics. Reading direction Left-to-Right. Facing Pages on.
- [ ] Set pages 4–5 as a facing pair. Set the last story page (8) as a single page, so it lands after a page turn, as KDP's own tip describes. Confirm in the Previewer that the cover and title page do not shift the pairing.
- [ ] Guided View: auto-detect needs panels that do not touch and borders distinct from the background. Keep gutters a flat colour; set the mask colour to match the gutters.
- [ ] Guided View order follows `ISSUE_01_PRODUCTION.md` §6: 1.1; 2.1–2.3; 3.1–3.3; 4.1–4.2; 5.1–5.3; 6.1–6.3; 7.1–7.2; 8.1. Splash pages get one selection.
- [ ] Panel 6.1 selection zooms to at least 200% (tiny bubble).
- [ ] Previewer: Phone, horizontal. Every capital at least 2 mm when magnified; no overflow; order correct.
- [ ] Save the `.kcb` project and keep it. File > Export makes the KPF; that is the upload file.

**Covers**

- [ ] Marketing cover: 1600 × 2560 px (width × height), JPEG, RGB, 300 ppi, 5 MB or less. Crop from the cover master the same way as the pages.
- [ ] The cover shows the title and author or contributor information. The text must match the detail page (§5, S1 and S2).
- [ ] No placeholder left anywhere: no `[NAME: …]`, `[YEAR: …]` or `[DATE: …]`.
- [ ] No price or promotion on the cover.
- [ ] Internal cover (first page of the KPF) matches the marketing cover and fills at least 50% of its space. Do not add a second cover page.
- [ ] The cover is dark, so the grey-border tip for light covers does not apply.

**Setup (drafts only; nothing submitted by this card)**

- [ ] Metadata as §2. No `⚡️` in any field, including file names.
- [ ] AI-content question answered truthfully.
- [ ] No link or call to action for Patreon or any other store inside the book or the description.
- [ ] Final sweep: `ISSUE_01_PRODUCTION.md` §8 Rejects and §10 final sweep.
