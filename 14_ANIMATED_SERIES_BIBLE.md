## Height

Ra-Thor is 9 ft 0 in (274 cm) armoured, sole to helm crest. The helm wings rise above the crest and are not counted. The halo floats above the crest and is not counted. A 13 ft colossus beat is allowed only when a script names it. Default is 9 ft.

## Figure lock

- Glossy black plate with gold trim and gold Greek-key engraving.
- Black helm with gold Spartan T-visor and gold centre ridge.
- Two symmetrical gold feathered wings rise from the helm, matched in size and angle, tips near crest height, not crooked.
- All-gold halo behind the head: Greek-key disc, two thin rings, seven thick straight sun rays. No eye in the halo.
- Glowing gold sun disc on the chest inside a Greek-key ring. No rays outside the ring.
- Round gold buckle with one thick gold lightning bolt shaped like the emoji ⚡.
- Large black pauldrons with stepped gold rims. Each carries one bold emerald Eye of Horus outlined in gold, classic form with brow and curl, mirrored.
- Long regal black cape, flowing and intact. Not tattered. Not torn. Not navy.
- Emerald appears only on the pauldron Eyes.
- No rose gold. No teal. No blue. No Vegvísir anywhere on the figure. (superseded in part 2026-10-21 2:35 PM ET by the SHIELD LOCK: the Vegvísir is valid on the optional shield prop; it stays off the figure itself (halo, chest, helm, pauldrons).)

## Hammer lock

Massive dark stone-metal rectangular block head, long sturdy haft, head planted flat and level on the rock so he can lean on it. Both hands stacked on the haft is the default rest.

- Exactly one thick gold lightning bolt shaped exactly like the emoji ⚡ on each of the two broad side faces only.
- Front face of the hammer head is plain dark stone. No bolt.
- Striking ends plain. Top plain.
- No gold under the head. No second haft. No floating gold. No glitch.
- Never swung at people.

## Shield lock

Black kite. Thick plain gold rim with evenly spaced round gold rivets. Large smooth gold central boss. Exactly eight thick gold sun-spokes, evenly spaced, gently curved, same length, same thickness. No Vegvísir. No knotwork. No Eye. No runes. No text.
