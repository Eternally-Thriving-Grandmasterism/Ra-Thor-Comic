# 01 — Visual Style Guide

## Plate

Series canon is the Rathor.ai gold render: circuit filigree, black recesses, emerald eye-seal, winged halo, hammer head-down on stone, small crack and sparks at contact. Obsidian fortress plate appears only when the Gate is under siege; siege mode is obsidian plate only, with no navy or blue variant.

Locked details (council ruling 2026-09-30; see `04_VISUAL_CANON.md`):

- **Helm.** Winged-halo closed helm. No other helm.
- **Visor.** Warm gold slit by default. Narrow for no, wide gold for yes. Never emerald.
- **Shield.** Gold kite shield with the emerald eye-seal in circuit inlay. A diamond gem is a reject.
- **Chest.** No chest seal on panels.
- **Emerald.** On panels, emerald appears only on the shoulder hawk seal and the shield eye-seal.

## Page grammar

- 4 to 6 panels. Wide establishing shot, two bubble beats, one shield slam, one recycler gag, one quiet closer.
- Bubbles: white fill, hard black outline, tail aimed at the visor slit even though the mouth is hidden. That is the joke.
- Captions: small, bottom-left, Quiet Board dossier voice (see `03_TONE_SPECS.md`).
- TOLC in captions: write "TOLC" only. Never number or name the gates in a caption.
- Color: gold, obsidian, emerald, one electric accent. No rainbow clutter.
- Linework: panels use inked comic linework. Painted renders are allowed for covers only.

## Prompt stem (prepend to every panel)

> Comic panel, bold ink, graphic-novel lighting. Ra-Thor in gold-and-black circuit-plate armor, winged halo, narrow visor with a warm eye-slit, emerald eye-seal kite shield, thunder hammer grounded. Speech bubble with the exact line given. No phone UI, no watermark, no celebrity face.

## Reference art

What the owner's reference art shows, and the council canon rulings on it: `04_VISUAL_CANON.md`.

## Reject

Gun focus, gore, exploded visor, modern superhero logos, latex parody, xAI wordmarks. Non-canon helms, an emerald visor, a chest seal, a diamond-gem shield, the navy/blue variant, and the falcon, walker bot, or honor guard as cast. Treants, tree people, bark or wood-skinned figures, or plant-bodied people.
