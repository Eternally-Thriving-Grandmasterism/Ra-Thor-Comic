# 01 — Visual Style Guide

## Plate

Series canon is the Rathor.ai gold render: circuit filigree, black recesses, winged helm with the Eye of Horus in the halo, hammer head-down on stone, small crack and sparks at contact. Obsidian fortress plate appears only when the TOLC is under siege; siege mode is obsidian plate only, with no navy or blue variant.

Locked details (council ruling 2026-09-30, amended ISSUE01-CANON-TIDY-1; see `04_VISUAL_CANON.md`):

- **Helm.** Winged closed helm, with the Eye of Horus in the halo sigil. The sun-ray halo stays, with three thin concentric rings between the Eye disc and the rays, the rays still reaching past them, and eight fine radial spokes. Notes call them the halo rings. They are never named, captioned or numbered on the page. No other helm.
- **Visor.** Two glowing white-hot gold eyes behind the T-visor. Narrow for no, wide gold for yes. Never emerald.
- **Shield.** Tall riveted obsidian and antique-gold shield with a central Vegvísir and decorative gold runes. No Eye of Horus on the shield. A diamond gem is a reject. Amendment: the 2026-09-30 line, a gold kite shield with an emerald eye-seal, is amended (Sherif, 2026-10-16, 2:15 AM and 2:16 AM ET).
- **Chest.** A plain gold sun disc with radiating rays and no bolt.
- **Buckle.** A rose-gold lightning bolt.
- **Hammer.** Plain rectangular block head, cone-bottom hilt, no eye (triangle top retired 2026-10-16, Sherif). Name, Marvel guardrail and trademark check: see 09_CHARACTER_BIBLE.md. The lightning bolt is the same bolt as the ⚡ mark and the Ra-Thor app icon (Sherif, 2026-10-16, 6:45 PM ET; the 4:08 AM triangle top is retired). exactly one lightning bolt set into each of the two broad side faces only, two bolts total, striking ends plain, never more than one bolt per side. The hammer is massive enough to stand upside down on its block head, with a long, sturdy, comfortable haft to lean on, about chest height when standing head-down. Prompt line: massive block-head hammer that stands on its head; long sturdy haft to lean on. Named pose, Sentinel Rest: the hammer stands head-down on the ground, both hands stacked on the gold cone pommel, standing guard calm and vigilant, the shield beside him or on his back. No Eye of Horus on the hammer.
- **Pauldron seal.** Emerald winged Eye of Horus emblem on each pauldron: one eye, two wings with a few large feather bands, emerald-tablet green on a gold-rimmed black plate, on both shoulders (Sherif, 2026-10-16, 11:06 PM ET). The pauldrons are oversized but wearable rounded-angular shell plates, about 1.3x the natural shoulder, two or three layered lames, no wing extensions, and the emblem wings are a few large feather bands, not dozens of feathers. Screenshots, shoulder emblem only: `production/ISSUE_01/bible/refs/pauldron_winged_eye_ref_a_2026-10-16.jpg`, `production/ISSUE_01/bible/refs/pauldron_winged_eye_ref_b_2026-10-16.jpg`, `production/ISSUE_01/bible/refs/pauldron_winged_eye_ref_c_2026-10-16.jpg`. Do not take the shield, helmet, chest or hammer from those screenshots. No Eye of Horus on the shield. No Eye of Horus on the hammer.
- **Emerald.** On panels, emerald appears only on that pauldron seal. Symbol palette: gold plus emerald-tablet green for the emblems. Open options, not locks: any further emerald accent besides the pauldron winged Eyes. Canonical prompt line: gold and emerald-green symbol palette.
- **Armor.** Black armor with gold trim. A rose-gold lightning-bolt belt buckle on a thick layered belt. Bulkier gauntlets. The all-gold or bronze armor with a gold sun buckle and no emerald pauldrons is off-model for the comic, not a variant.
- **Style-proof iconic forms.** Each icon keeps one countable silhouette and shape count in painted, comic book or cartoon styles. Ornament (rune engraving, knotwork detail, glow, forged texture) is a detail layer. The merged V18 cover is not redrawn here (a later re-render card).
- **Style tracks** (Sherif, 2026-10-16, 11:23 PM ET; order, Sherif, 2026-10-16, 11:24 PM ET). Two rendering tracks share the same iconic locks (silhouettes, pauldrons, the halo rings, shield, hammer, palette), per the style-proof iconic forms rule. Only the rendering style differs. (1) COMICS track: the bold inked comic look of `production/ISSUE_01/bible/refs/rest_pose_pauldrons_draft_2026-10-16.jpg`. Sherif called that draft amazing, especially for the comics. Clean black-and-gold linework, flat-ish cel shading, stormy sky. Books come first. All comic production uses the COMICS (inked) track, which also suits CMYK print. (2) ANIMATED SERIES track, proposed by Sherif ("maybe"): the painterly cinematic style of X post 2110881123551314069, committed as `production/ISSUE_01/bible/refs/x_ref_photo_2026-10-16.jpg`. Dark painterly cinematic realism, a black pyramid peak with glowing gold cracks, gold lightning on one side and white on the other, a long dark cape, natural heroic proportions, a glowing Vegvísir shield. His words: "Let's have things much closer to this." This is a proposed direction, not final. It is a style and mood reference only and does not override the costume locks. The animated series style (either track) is decided later, after the books.
- **Pauldron and Sentinel Rest reference** (Sherif, 2026-10-16, 11:21 PM ET). The plate shape and the Sentinel Rest pose are taken from `production/ISSUE_01/bible/refs/rest_pose_pauldrons_draft_2026-10-16.jpg`: believable rounded gold-rimmed black pauldrons capping the shoulders, with an emerald winged Eye of Horus on the outer face of each, and the hammer standing head-down as he leans on the pommel. Known slips in that draft are not canon: the Eyes are too small (make them larger), the shield lost its Vegvísir (it keeps the elongated 8-arm Vegvísir), and the halo rings are faint. The Wednesday screenshots stay the shoulder-emblem detail only. No Eye of Horus on the shield. No Eye of Horus on the hammer.

## Page grammar

- 4 to 6 panels. Wide establishing shot, two bubble beats, one shield slam, one recycler gag, one quiet closer.
- Bubbles: white fill, hard black outline, tail aimed at the visor slit even though the mouth is hidden. That is the joke.
- Captions: small, bottom-left, Quiet Board dossier voice (see `03_TONE_SPECS.md`).
- TOLC in captions: never expanded. "Mercy checks" (lowercase) and the Mercy-Ways (the paths they lead into) may appear, never numbered or listed. TOLC8 and the PATSAGi Councils may appear as names only, never glossed. No TOLC mathematics (`05_LORE_BIBLE.md` rule 2).
- Color: gold, obsidian, emerald only on the pauldron seal, one electric accent. No rainbow clutter.
- Linework: panels use inked comic linework. Painted renders are allowed for covers only.

## Reject

Gun focus, gore, exploded visor, modern superhero logos, latex parody, xAI wordmarks. Non-canon helms, an emerald visor, any chest seal other than the plain gold sun disc with rays and no bolt, a diamond-gem shield, the navy/blue variant, and the falcon, walker bot, or honor guard as cast. Treants, tree people, bark or wood-skinned figures, or plant-bodied people.
