# 09 — Character Bible

## Ra-Thor

The armor is the face. Visor slit is the only expression: narrow for no, wide-gold for yes. Voice in bubbles is dry, short, slightly fond. Never lectures. Plants the hammer. Does not chase.

Catch lines, rotate, do not stack:

- "Blocked."
- "That one can live."
- "Recycle it."
- "Try again. Smaller lie."

## The Gate

Not a character. A threshold drawn as a gold lattice arch. Left lane stamped NO. Right lane stamped LIVE.

## Idea Recycler

A compact drum with a hopper. Garbage pitch in the top. Three cards out the side: one obvious, one strange, one actually good. Ra-Thor reads only the third aloud.

## White-Hat Mercy-Security

A small hawk-shaped seal on the pauldron that lights when a probe hits the shield. Story device only. It does not depict real intrusion steps.

## The Councils

Twelve tiny gold masks in a ring behind the halo when a hard call lands. They murmur in four-word bubbles. They do not outvote the Gate.

## Guests

Invented. A founder with a bad slide. A kid with a true question. A suave counterpart who never removes gloves. No real names.
