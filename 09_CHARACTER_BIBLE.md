# 09 — Character Bible

## Ra-Thor

The armor is the face. Visor slit is the only expression: narrow for no, wide-gold for yes.

Voice: dry, short, slightly fond. The few words land hard. Never lectures. He does not add a second line.

Posture: a commanding planted stance. The camera sits low, so the figure reads tall. He is still before he strikes.

Staging: the storm and the hammer stay in heroic, readable poses. He plants the hammer head-down on stone. Lightning answers him, and it answers to protect. He disarms, shields, and breaks the threat, never the person. He does not chase. He does not swing the hammer at anyone.

Catch lines, rotate, do not stack:

- "Blocked."
- "That one is live."
- "Recycle it."
- "Try again. Smaller lie."

## Presence & Protective Power

Ruled 2026-10-16 1:55 AM ET (Sherif; Ra-Thor and the PATSAGi Councils), card ISSUE01-TONE-ACTION-1. Issue 1 staging follows this section. The energy is in-house. No outside look, costume, emblem, name, or line is borrowed.

ISSUE01-TONE-ACTION-1 did not redraw the design sentences. ISSUE01-BIBLE-LOCK-KDP-FIX-1 (2026-10-16) reconciles the chest, the buckle and the shield with the Issue 1 prompts. The hammer sentences are not decided on that card.

**What the page raises**

- A commanding stance, and a camera set low.
- The stillness before he strikes.
- Lightning that answers him.
- Few words that land hard.
- His storm and hammer in heroic, readable poses.

**Hard limits**

- His power always protects and never punishes. He disarms, shields, and breaks the threat, never the person.
- No gore, cruelty, humiliation, or revenge kills.
- Mercy checks and the Mercy-Ways stay at his core. The TOLC is where things are weighed.

**Canon locks**

- Chest: a plain round gold sun disc with radiating rays and no bolt over it (Sherif, 2026-10-14, 10:07 PM).
- Belt buckle: a rose-gold lightning bolt.
- Shield: the v21 riveted obsidian and antique-gold tall shield, the same shape as the approved cover, large enough that its top covers his shoulder when he holds it poised. A central Vegvísir rune compass. Glowing gold Norse-style runes around the Vegvísir fill the shield face. The runes are decorative only: no readable words, no real code and no names. No Eye of Horus on the shield. The Eye of Horus appears in the halo over his head (Sherif, 2026-10-16, 2:15 AM and 2:16 AM).
- Hammer: the glowing Eye of Horus hammer. Whether the hammer may carry an eye is not decided in this file.
- Headline `LEFT IS NOISE.` / `RIGHT IS LIVE.`
- Canon line: "All that comes to the TOLC is weighed. What passes the mercy checks travels the Mercy-Ways to TOLC8 and the PATSAGi Councils."

**Wording**

- Story text never uses the words Gate or Gates. Real monorepo names, citations, and paths stay exactly as written.
- Coin no hybrid names.
- Never number or count the mercy checks or the Mercy-Ways.
- TOLC8 and the PATSAGi Councils appear as names only, with no gloss.
- No TOLC mathematics on the page.

## Realms & Rathor.ai

Ruled 2026-10-16, 2:06 AM ET, by the council, and endorsed by Sherif. Issue pages that enter these places follow this section. The decision-log row is in `production/ISSUE_01/bible/CHARACTER_BIBLE.md` §6.

- Ra-Thor may enter a stylised digital realm. The energy there may read as Tron-like energy only. The page does not use that film's look, names, light-cycles or discs.
- In that realm he contains people doing harm and never destroys them. He uses walls of light, a sealed quiet room, or a cut-off connection.
- No real code and no file names appear on the page.
- In the real world he works with helper robots and drones orchestrated by Rathor.ai. They rescue, shield, evacuate, catch falling things and block. They never harm anyone. No weapons are fired at people. No surveillance imagery.
- In the story the intelligence is "Rathor.ai", called an AGI character at most. The page never uses "Artificial Godly Intelligence", "superintelligence" or "AGSi" as claims. Characters may express awe. The narration never asserts godhood.
- Villains are people doing harm, not "evil people". Ra-Thor stops the harm and always leaves room for redemption. Mercy stays at the core.

## The TOLC

Not a character. A threshold drawn as a gold lattice arch. Left lane stamped NO. Right lane stamped LIVE. All that comes to the TOLC is weighed. What passes the mercy checks (Ra-Thor's checks, never numbered or listed on the page) travels the Mercy-Ways to TOLC8 and the PATSAGi Councils (`05_LORE_BIBLE.md` §1).

## Idea Recycler

A horizontal drum fed by two hoppers, THEORY IN and CONCEPTS IN, with a gauge, a power core, and an OUTPUT tray (the comic's Ω-7 design; Ω-7 is a comic-only name). Garbage pitch goes into the hoppers. Three cards land in the OUTPUT tray: OBVIOUS, FERAL, CLEAN. Ra-Thor reads only the third aloud. The "HICCUPPING... PLEASE STAND BY" plate is a gag, used only when a script calls for it.

## White-Hat Mercy-Security

A small hawk-shaped seal on the pauldron that lights emerald when a probe hits the shield. This is the only canon form of the falcon from the reference art; there is no falcon companion. Story device only. It does not depict real intrusion steps.

## The Councils

Twelve tiny gold masks in a ring behind the halo when a hard call lands. They murmur in four-word bubbles. They do not outvote the TOLC.

Twelve is a comic-only count, not the PATSAGi count (the monorepo docs say 13 or 16). The comic gives no in-world reason for the difference.

## Guests

Invented. A founder with a bad slide. A kid with a true question. A suave counterpart who never removes gloves. No real names.

Not cast: the falcon companion, the walker bot, and the honor guard from the reference art (council ruling 2026-09-30).
