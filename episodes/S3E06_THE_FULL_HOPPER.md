# Season 3, Episode 06 — The Full Hopper

Status: draft. Six panels scripted, not yet prompted. Season 3 finale, arc B, The Full Hopper. Lab Night mode (lead), with one Snap panel (Panel 5, the button, in the Threshold's LIVE lane) as the one borrowed panel. The Recycler's hopper still runs low. At the Threshold, a fresh guest offers a painted hopper that only looks full. Ra-Thor drops an honest part into it, and the part skids off paint: there is no room inside. The painted hopper is blocked. Then the recurring Tollkeeper leads the Tollkeepers in choosing to open their crates (05), and they bring them through the LIVE lane themselves. In Recycler Bay they hand their own crate locks to the Recycler, and the real hopper fills, part by part. The Founder's notebook, shown as an object, carries the season's only notebook line. The season ends in Recycler Bay with the hammer grounded.

## Panel 4 — The block, at the Threshold

Caption: THRESHOLD. AN HONEST PART, DROPPED IN, SKIDS OFF PAINT. THE PAINTED HOPPER IS BLOCKED. THE DAUBER IS NOT.
Bubble: "Blocked."
Without a word, the Watcher holds out a single plain spare part from a grey glove. Ra-Thor takes it and drops it into the painted hopper's mouth. It does not sink in: it skids across the flat painted board and clatters to the stone. There is no room inside a hopper that is only painted full. Ra-Thor sets the painted hopper down gently on the NO side of the arch, shield upright between it and the LIVE lane, and hands the part back to the Watcher. The Dauber steps back, unharmed, paper mask tilted. Hammer head-down on stone, untouched.

- **Panel 3 (543-pad section).** The groove drops out for the decision. A held pad on F# minor to C# minor with the 543 top over the 108 Hz drone, and the twelve-mask blip ring murmuring across the stereo field.

Then the Grounded motif on Ra-Thor's lead synth over the bubbly bass: four notes rise and land back on the tonic, A, and hold, over a held A-major pad with the 543 top and the 108 Hz drone.
