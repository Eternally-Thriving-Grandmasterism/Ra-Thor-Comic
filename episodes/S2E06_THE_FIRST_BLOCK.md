# Season 2, Episode 06 — The First Block

Status: draft. Six panels scripted, not yet prompted. Season 2 finale, arc C, The Lamp Before the Gate. Quiet Board mode (lead), with a Night Watch object-flashback opener as the one borrowed panel. Ra-Thor turns to the notebook's last written page: a sketch of the lattice arch, and after it a blank page. He carries the notebook to the Threshold, where Season 0 began, and is revealed under the arch with his hammer already grounded. In the present, he does what 05 records as his first act: he turns away a piece of noise, kindly, and lets one small honest thing through. The noise goes to the Recycler, a single seedling passes, and the season ends at the Gate with the hammer grounded.

The Founder: implied only (05, Q3). His lamp, his notebook, his desk and his empty chair, in the flashback only. No figure, no hands, no silhouette, never in a bubble. No handwritten line in this episode. One implied-Founder reading line only, Ra-Thor's in Panel 1, about the page.

The reveal (05 Era IV; council ruling, PR #26): Ra-Thor under the lattice arch, in the present day, Panel 2, with 05's Era IV first caption, verbatim. Nothing is restaged as a flashback of Ra-Thor (arc lock §2).

Guests (invented, no house, no faction, no glyph, no real name, no celebrity face, no logo, no real place or culture):
- **The Loopwinder.** Long dark coat, gloves never removed, face always hidden inside a deep hood wound from glittering ribbon. Carries a big hoop of glittering ribbon printed over and over with MORE. LOUDER. AGAIN.
- **The Sower.** Plain undyed smock, a wide woven hat tipped low, a scarf across the lower face, face kept in shadow the whole time. Carries one seedling in a small clay cup.

Set: the Quiet Room (one table, one practical lamp, the Quiet Board on the far wall) and the Threshold (dark stone, gold lattice arch, left lane NO, right lane LIVE, a compact Idea Recycler beside the arch, a low stone ledge at the foot of the arch).
