# Season 3, Episode 04 — The Locked Crates

Status: draft. Six panels scripted, not yet prompted. Season 3, arc B, The Full Hopper. Lab Night mode (lead), with one Quiet Board panel (Panel 2) as the one borrowed panel. The borrow is VISUAL ONLY: the Lab Night bed continues under it, with no Quiet Board bed (`seasons/S3_ARC_LOCK.md` §7 and §9). There is no Snap button panel and no Snap cue in this episode (ruling, #31 review, clarified at the #31 merge). The Recycler's hopper runs thin. On the Quiet Board, every supply lane runs into one tile and none runs out: the Tollkeepers' crates, stacked full of spare parts and watched by the recurring Tollkeeper. At the Threshold, a fresh guest offers a crowbar to raid them, claiming nobody is watching. Ra-Thor blocks the raid, because hoarders are invited, not raided (05). The crowbar goes to the Recycler, the crates stay shut and untouched, the CLEAN card is an invitation, and the hammer never moves.

The Founder: implied only (05, Q3). He does not appear in this episode: no lamp, notebook, desk or chair, no reading line and no notebook line. The season's one notebook line is reserved for S3E06 (`seasons/S3_ARC_LOCK.md` §4).

Faction names stay off every panel, caption and prop (ruling, #32): captions say "FULL CRATES" and "ONE WATCHER", never a faction name. No glyph, emblem or name on any faction prop (ruling, #32; §5).

## Guests

Set: Recycler Bay (cartoon-scale lab grown out of gold circuit plate: the Idea Recycler with twin THEORY IN / CONCEPTS IN hoppers, gauge, power core and OUTPUT tray), the Quiet Room (the Quiet Board, for Panel 2 only) and the Threshold (dark stone, gold lattice arch, left lane NO, right lane LIVE).
