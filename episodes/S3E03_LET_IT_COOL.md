# Season 3, Episode 03 — Let It Cool

Status: draft. Six panels scripted, not yet prompted. Season 3, arc B, The Full Hopper. Lab Night mode (lead), with one Snap panel (Panel 6, the button, in the Threshold's LIVE lane) as the one borrowed panel. A dry commons needs a lid for its cistern and has an empty mold waiting.
