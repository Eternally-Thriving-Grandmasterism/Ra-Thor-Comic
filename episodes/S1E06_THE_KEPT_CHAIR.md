# Season 1, Episode 06 — The Kept Chair

Status: draft. Six panels scripted, not yet prompted. Season 1, arc A finale (six-episode plan, Q10 open), The Long Table. Suave Hour mode (lead). The last houses arrive together, every chair stands at one height, and the houses already spoken for finally take their seats. One new arrival tries to claim the shadowed chair as a spare. The claim is blocked, the chair is kept open and lit for the house that has not answered, the arrival trades the claim for a CLEAN card and takes their own seat, and the hammer never moves.

Guests: the envoys already spoken for, faces hidden exactly as before. No real names, no celebrity faces, no logos, no real places or cultures.

Hammer untouched, still grounded.
