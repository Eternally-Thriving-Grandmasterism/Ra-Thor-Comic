# Season 3, Episode 02 — One Tool, Many Hands

Status: draft. Six panels scripted, not yet prompted. Season 3, arc B, The Full Hopper. Lab Night mode (lead), with one Snap panel (Panel 6, the button) as the one borrowed panel. A dry commons has one good wrench and too many hands waiting for it. At the Threshold, a guest offers a padlock that lets the tool open only for a blank token. Passed from hand to hand, the lock opens for none of the hands that need the tool. The lock is blocked and the tool is kept. The lock goes to the Recycler, the CLEAN card becomes a shared tool wall with a hook for every hand, and the hammer never moves.

The Founder: implied only (05, Q3). He does not appear in this episode: no lamp, notebook, desk or chair, no reading line and no notebook line. The season's one notebook line is reserved for S3E06 (`seasons/S3_ARC_LOCK.md` §4).

Faction names stay off every panel, caption and prop (ruling, #32): captions say "A DRY COMMONS". No glyph, emblem or name on any faction prop (ruling, #32).

Guests (invented, faces always hidden, no glyph, no emblem, no house, no real name, no celebrity face, no logo, no real place or culture):
- **The Mender (from the dry commons).** Plain canvas work apron, face always hidden behind a dark smoked-glass work mask. Holds the commons' one good tool: a plain wrench with a worn grip. The wrench is unmarked.
- **The Latchwright.** Long dark coat with a tall stiff collar turned up over the nose, a deep knit cap pulled down to the brows, eyes lost in shadow. Carries a heavy gilded padlock with a blank token slot, and one blank token on a short chain. Lock and token are unmarked: no currency, number or symbol.
- **The waiting hands (from the dry commons).** Shown only as bare and gloved hands reaching in from the frame edges, no faces, no bodies past the wrist, never counted.

Set: Recycler Bay (cartoon-scale lab grown out of gold circuit plate: the Idea Recycler with twin THEORY IN / CONCEPTS IN hoppers, gauge, power core and OUTPUT tray) and the Threshold (dark stone, gold lattice arch, left lane NO, right lane LIVE). Blocks happen at the Threshold; blocked items go to Recycler Bay afterward (`seasons/S3_ARC_LOCK.md` §5).

## Panel 4 — The block, at the Threshold

Caption: THRESHOLD. PASSED HAND TO HAND, IT OPENS FOR NONE OF THEM. THE LOCK IS BLOCKED. THE LATCHWRIGHT IS NOT.

At the end of the row, Ra-Thor takes the lock back and sets it down gently on the NO side of the arch, shield upright between the lock and the LIVE lane. The wrench stays free in the Mender's hand. The Latchwright steps back, unharmed. Hammer head-down on stone, untouched.
