# Season 3, Episode 01 — The Last Scraps

Status: draft. Six panels scripted, not yet prompted. Season 3 opener, arc B, The Full Hopper. Lab Night mode (lead), with one Snap panel (Panel 6, the button) as the one borrowed panel. A dry commons brings its last scraps to Recycler Bay. At the Threshold, a guest offers a gilded jar that promises water from nowhere. Lifting the jar shows where the water really comes from. The jar is blocked, the jar and the scraps go into the Recycler, the CLEAN card becomes a cloth rain-catcher the Hollow sews for itself, and the hammer never moves.

The Founder: implied only (05, Q3). He does not appear in this episode: no lamp, notebook, desk or chair, no reading line and no notebook line. The season's one notebook line is reserved for S3E06 (`seasons/S3_ARC_LOCK.md` §4).

Guests (invented, faces always hidden, no glyph, no emblem, no house, no real name, no celebrity face, no logo, no real place or culture):
- **The Gleaner (from the dry commons).** Patched undyed shawl drawn over the head, a scarf across the lower face, face kept in shadow the whole time. Carries a plain cloth sack of scraps: bent tin, frayed cloth, short lengths of cord. The sack is unmarked.
- **The Jar-Bearer.** Long oilskin coat, gloves never removed, face always hidden behind a round brass helmet with one fogged faceplate. Carries a gilded jar on a small gilded base. The jar is unmarked.

Set: Recycler Bay (cartoon-scale lab grown out of gold circuit plate: the Idea Recycler with twin THEORY IN / CONCEPTS IN hoppers, gauge, power core and OUTPUT tray) and the Threshold (dark stone, gold lattice arch, left lane NO, right lane LIVE). Blocks happen at the Threshold; blocked items go to Recycler Bay afterward (`seasons/S3_ARC_LOCK.md` §5).

## Panel 4 — The block, at the Threshold

Caption: THRESHOLD. UNDER THE JAR, A THIN TUBE BACK TO THE COMMONS. THE JAR IS BLOCKED. THE JAR-BEARER IS NOT.
Bubble: "Blocked."
With one gauntlet, Ra-Thor lifts the jar a hand's width off its gilded base. Underneath, a thin gold tube runs out of the base, down through a seam in the stone, and away along the road toward the dry commons: the water was never from nowhere. He pinches the tube closed between two armored fingers, sets the jar down gently on the NO side of the arch, and lays the shield upright between the jar and the LIVE lane. The Jar-Bearer steps back, unharmed, faceplate fogging. Hammer head-down on stone, untouched.
