# Season 3, Episode 05 — The Better Deal

Status: draft. Six panels scripted, not yet prompted. Season 3, arc B, The Full Hopper. Lab Night mode (lead), with one Snap panel (Panel 6, the button, in the Threshold's LIVE lane) as the one borrowed panel. A dry commons brings a bare water-wheel frame with no paddles. At the Threshold, the recurring Tollkeeper offers a spare paddle for a blank toll tag. Ra-Thor fits the lone paddle to the bare wheel: it rocks once and stops, because one paddle at a time never turns a wheel. He blocks the toll, not the Tollkeeper. The Tollkeeper puts the blank tags and the paddle into the Recycler by choice, the CLEAN card shows a better deal (05), the paddle in the shared hopper becomes a water wheel both sides use, and the hammer never moves.

## Panel 4 — The block, at the Threshold

Caption: THRESHOLD. A PADDLE FOR A TAG: THE WHEEL ROCKS ONCE AND STOPS. THE TOLL IS BLOCKED. THE WATCHER IS NOT.

Then he sets the ring of blank tags down gently on the NO side of the arch, shield upright between the tags and the LIVE lane. The Tollkeeper stands calm, unharmed, lantern steady, holding the paddle. Hammer head-down on stone, untouched.
