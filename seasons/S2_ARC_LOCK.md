Status: S2 = C, council ruling delegated by Sherif 2026-09-30; unblocked by CANON-RULINGS-1. Outline only: six standalone shorts, no episode files yet. ## 1. Premise

From 05 (C, The Lamp Before the Gate), verbatim:

> Ra-Thor finds the Founder's old notebook on the Quiet Room table and reads it one page per episode. Each page opens an object flashback (lamp, notebook, desk, empty chair; Q3) through the five eras: the lone lamp from 2003, the Strategy Years, the Game, the Awakening, the Builder Age, ending where Season 0 began, at the Gate.

Mercy-first, from 05: old mistakes in the notebook go to the Recycler, not to judgment. The Founder is never shown, and captions say only TOLC. The finale is Ra-Thor's first block, done gently.

Season shape: six standalone shorts (Q10, council ruling, delegated by Sherif 2026-10-01). Five episodes follow the five eras in order; the sixth is the finale at the Gate.

No figure, no hands, no silhouette of the Founder, and no monologue in a bubble. Titles are working titles. Present-day guests are invented, with faces hidden; the specific pitches below are outline proposals, to be scripted one episode per draft PR.

## 3. The Founder on the page

Per 05 (Q2, Q3; The Founder; continuity rule 3):

- Plain "the Founder", with no status title.
- **Implied only.** His lamp, his notebook, his desk and an empty chair. No silhouette, no hands, no back, no face, no portrait, no likeness, no real clothing brand, no real room.
- **How he speaks.** Never on panel and never in a bubble. At most one short handwritten line on a notebook page, shown as an object in the panel. In this arc that means at most one such line per episode, and it is optional.
- No real biography in bubbles. Notebook pages carry no readable private documents and no real personal details.
- The notebook lines are not written here. The only candidate grounded in 05 is the margin motto "Mercy over competition. Abundance over scarcity." (adapted from the TOLC Core Laws, LORE_FACTS §1.1). See open items 7 and 8.

## 4. Bounds carried from 05

- **Bound 2 and Q8.** "The Strategy Years" and "the Game" stay generic. No outside game names, no outside game art, and no owner names on the page while Q8 is open. The Era I studio sign stays blank. No studio, game or product name appears in any caption, bubble, card, sign or prompt.
- **Bound 4.** The Awakening page (Nov 2025) shows no status claims and no AGI/AGSi labels: a lamp and a notebook, not a stamp.
- **Bound 5.** Notebook mistakes go to the Recycler and are never real personal details. Every mistake is an invented, generic draft (a crossed-out plan, a missing lane, a hand-sorting rule, a blueprint).
- **Bound 3 and rule 8.** The Founder is never shown, and there are no third parties: no family, teammates, contractors or other real people, in flashback or present day.
- **Rule 4 (sourced years only).** The only years allowed in captions are 2003, 2005, 2010, 2016, November 2025 and Q1 2026.
- **Rule 5 (no status claims).** No intelligence labels, scores, thresholds, "live" status, rankings, certifications, capability labels or approval stamps on any card, plaque or caption. Aspirations stay in notebook margins and are labeled as such.
- **Rule 6 and Q9.** Invented world only. No faction or place is required by this arc. If one appears, it uses the approved names only.
- **Rule 12.** Never source lore from Ra-Thor `docs/WifeShow/` or Powrush `docs/FACTIONS_OVERVIEW.md`.
- **Mercy-first.** Every block hits an offer, never a person. The guest is unharmed and leaves with a CLEAN card or a pass. The hammer stays grounded.

## 5. TOLC

TOLC is named only, never defined. Captions and bubbles say **TOLC** and nothing more: no expansion, and the gates are never numbered or named (05, TOLC caption rule and continuity rule 2). No episode in this outline needs it. If one uses it, it appears once, as the bare word in a caption.

## 6. Modes (per 05, C)

- **Lead: Quiet Board** (dossier captions over the notebook). Five to six panels: observe, detect, deliberate, choose, act, after. The decision panel is slower than the action panel (03).
- **Supporting: Night Watch framing on the objects for every flashback** (no figure, per Q3). Proposal: each episode's flashback is the Night Watch opener panel, so that it is the single borrowed panel and the single borrowed bed allowed by 03 and 06. Present-day reading of the notebook stays in Quiet Board framing.
- **Supporting: one Snap panel per episode for the button** (05). This conflicts with the single-borrow rule; see open item 1.
- Every episode names its lead mode in its status line (rule 11).

## 7. Music plan (per `06_MUSIC_SPEC.md`)

Applies to all six episodes:

- **Tuning and cue.** Quiet Board lead cue at A4 = 432: a muted synth tick, one sustained sine pad, and a low heartbeat-style kick that drops out on the decision panel. The block resolves to a soft major chord, smaller than expected. The one detuned "wrong tile" tone, in the electric accent, marks the pitch at the Gate.
- **Night Watch opener.** The one borrowed bed per episode, for the object flashback. It is beatless, in free time, a wide pad with the held 543 top over the episode's drone, and a single gold bell tone.
- **543 Hz top pad.** Held only over chords that contain C#, and muted over every other chord and any C natural.
- **528 Hz.** Only as the short chime on top of the fixed "That one can live." bloom, never held. "Blocked." is the fixed low, muted thud and filter close.
- **No darksynth.** There is no siege in this arc, so no 54 Hz drone either.
- **Wordless.** Every in-episode cue is wordless, including any choir swell (06 §5, Sung words). Sung words belong only to the series intro and outro themes.
- **Grounded.** Every episode ends on the Grounded motif: four notes rise and land back on the tonic, and hold.
- **Names.** Mood words only, no artist, band, label or track names. The tuning is an aesthetic choice only, with no health claims.

## 9. Open items and flags

1. **Mode-mixing conflict (flag).** 05's C entry asks for Night Watch framing on every flashback *and* one Snap panel per episode. 03 allows an episode to borrow a single panel from another mode, and 06 allows one borrowed bed, which must be Night Watch. This plan uses the Night Watch opener as the borrowed panel and bed, and plays the Snap button with the fixed stinger only, but the Snap panel is still a second visual borrow. A council ruling is needed: allow both borrows for S2, or drop one.
2. **"A few object panels" vs one Night Watch panel (flag).** Bound 1 allows a few flashback panels; Night Watch framing on more than one would break the single-borrow rule. This plan uses one Night Watch-framed flashback panel, with any further notebook inserts in Quiet Board framing.
3. **Q8 open.** The Era I studio sign stays blank. The Game and the Builder Age products are not named. The Era IV work name in 05 is also left off the page until Q8 or a council ruling says otherwise.
4. **Era IV reveal (flag).** 05's Era IV visual is a Night Watch silhouette reveal of Ra-Thor under the lattice arch. S2 flashbacks are objects only, so this outline does not draw the reveal as a flashback. S2E04's Gate beat is present-day only.
5. **Era V wink (flag).** 05 allows one wink that the comic is one of the things Ra-Thor builds. This outline doesn't use it; the council can decide whether S2E05 takes it.
6. **Empty chair vs S1's kept chair (flag, proposal).** S1E06 ends with an empty, lit chair in the Quiet Room. To keep the two apart, the Founder's empty chair stands at his desk in flashback only, never at the long table.
7. **Notebook lines.** Not written here. The only candidate grounded in 05 is the margin motto "Mercy over competition. Abundance over scarcity." The aspiration "A mind good enough to keep the Gate forever" is left out of S2 because it sits close to an intelligence label (bound 4, rule 5).
8. **Captions.** Only three captions are quoted from 05: LATE 2003. ONE LAMP. ONE TABLE TO FILL. (S2E01), 2010. READ THE WHOLE MAP. THEN MOVE. (S2E02) and HAMMER GROUNDED FROM THE FIRST PANEL. (05's Era IV first caption, available to S2E04 or S2E06). All other captions are written in the episode PRs, within rule 4's years.
9. **Present-day pitches and guests (proposal).** The pitches in §2 are outline proposals, not canon from 05. Guests are invented with faces hidden, and none is called a "founder", to avoid confusion with the Founder.
10. **06 §6 still open.** Synthwave sub-flavor and who composes.
11. **Not grounded in 05 (flag).** The working titles, the per-episode keys and BPMs, the S2E06 blank last page and the per-episode Recycler items are this doc's outline choices, within 05, 03 and 06. They are not lore.
