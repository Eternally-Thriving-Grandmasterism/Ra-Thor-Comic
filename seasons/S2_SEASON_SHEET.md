## 5. Rulings ledger (Season 2)

Council rulings applied across arc C, with where each shows in the repo.

1. **One borrow.** Each episode borrows one panel and one bed: the Night Watch object-flashback opener (P1). Snap gets the fixed stinger only, with no Snap panel and no Snap cue. Resolves arc lock open item 1. The one-borrow rule outranks 05's per-era visual modes (council ruling, PR #27): S2E05's flashback keeps Night Watch framing instead of 05's Lab Night mode, and S2E06's reveal is not drawn as a Night Watch silhouette.
2. **Era cards only as 05 gives them.** Small Quiet Board card in P1, year only as 05 gives it, short form (council ruling, PR #27): LATE 2003, 2010, 2016, NOVEMBER 2025 (approved month-year form, council ruling, PR #26), Q1 2026, with no "SINCE". Era III is year only: 2016. No day, month-day, place or name beside a card; never in a bubble; no years in present-day panels. S2E06 has no era card, because the arc lock gives it no era year.
3. **Era captions only where 05 gives one explicitly.** Never a paraphrase, never 05 prose promoted to a caption. Only E01 (ONE LAMP. ONE TABLE TO FILL.) and E02 (READ THE WHOLE MAP. THEN MOVE.) carry era captions, plus the Era IV caption in E06 (item 6). Applied by the fix commit on PR #25 and recorded in the reject lists of S2E03–S2E06.
4. **At most one implied-Founder reading line per episode.** About the object or the idea, never about him. Every S2 episode uses exactly one, spoken by Ra-Thor off panel in P1 (§3).
5. **The motto, used once.** "Mercy over competition. Abundance over scarcity." appears only in S2E01, as a handwritten notebook line shown as an object. No new Founder line was written in S2.
6. **The Era IV caption, used once.** HAMMER GROUNDED FROM THE FIRST PANEL. was reserved for S2E06 with the Ra-Thor reveal (council ruling, PR #26; noted in S2E05's "Next (for S2E06)" section). It appears once, verbatim, in a caption box with no bubble, on S2E06's present-day Panel 2: Ra-Thor full figure under the lattice arch, lit from the front by the lattice glow. It is not restaged as a flashback.
7. **The Era V wink, only in S2E05.** One quiet panel (S2E05 P2): a sheet of six empty comic panels with the arch inked in the first box. No claim, no title, not the button. No other episode has a wink; S2E04 and S2E06 also ban it in their reject lists.
8. **The series title only on packaging.** It never appears on story pages, panels or in-world props. It belongs only to packaging (cover, intro/outro title card, credits), and Q8 is untouched (council ruling, PR #27; recorded in S2E06's reject list).
9. **One wordless choir swell, in S2E06.** Under the pass, a single open vowel with no lyric and no word-forming syllables. S2E01–S2E05 have no choir. Sung words belong only to the series intro and outro themes (`06_MUSIC_SPEC.md` §5).
10. **The notebook stays whole.** The Recycler takes the episode's objects and, at most, a loose slip or sheet from the notebook. Two Recycler items are fine as long as the notebook stays whole (council ruling, PR #27).
11. **Q8 still open.** The Era I studio sign stays blank. No owner, studio, game, product or world names. The Strategy Years and the Game stay generic. No real IP, no real people, no third parties.
