## 3. Token continuity and the kept chair

**The running tally** (P6 captions, a running "spoken for" count only; no total of chairs or houses is ever stated):

- E01: ONE CHAIR SPOKEN FOR. THE REST STILL OPEN. HAMMER STILL GROUNDED.
- E02: TWO CHAIRS SPOKEN FOR. EVERY CHAIR THE SAME HEIGHT. HAMMER STILL GROUNDED.
- E03: THREE CHAIRS SPOKEN FOR. THE INK STAYS. HAMMER STILL GROUNDED.
- E04: FOUR CHAIRS SPOKEN FOR. MORE HOUSES ON THE ROAD. ONE HAS NOT ANSWERED.
- E05: FIVE CHAIRS SPOKEN FOR. ONE HOUSE STILL SILENT. HAMMER STILL GROUNDED.
- E06: EVERY HOUSE THAT CAME IS SEATED. ONE CHAIR KEPT. THE DOOR STAYS OPEN.

**Markers into the seating.** Each single-envoy episode leaves its house's marker on a chair, and each later episode's set carries the earlier markers forward (E02 shows the knot; E03 the knot and step; E04 the knot, step and pen; E05 the knot, step, pen and hinge). No one is seated in E01–E05. In E06 the houses already spoken for sit at the chairs their markers held, and each marker moves to the table in front of its house (P1). In P5 those envoys each tap their marker once on the wood: knot, step, pen, hinge, fan. The late houses set down their own small glyph tokens as they sit (P2).

**The kept chair thread.**

- E04, P6: one chair at the far end sits in shadow, untouched; the caption says ONE HAS NOT ANSWERED; the last mask glances at that chair.
- E05: the shadowed far chair is in the set from the start; in the Night Watch closer the parasol tips toward it for a beat; ONE HOUSE STILL SILENT; nobody sits.
- E06: the Cord Envoy tries to claim it as a spare (P2); the councils say "Kept open. Not spare." (P3); the claim is blocked and the shield lights the seat (P4); in P6 it stands empty and lit, never filled and never explained, the door open.
- The premise in `05_LORE_BIBLE.md` (LOCKED A) speaks of "one empty chair held open for a house that refused to come". The episodes only ever say the house has not answered (see Discrepancies).

## 7. Discrepancies noted across the episodes (not fixed here)

1. **"Refused" vs "has not answered."** 05's premise says the open chair is for "a house that refused to come"; E04–E06 say only that the house has not answered or is silent. The softer wording leaves the house's choice open.
2. **The Glass Envoy's screen size.** E04 calls it a "tall gilded folding screen"; E06 calls it "small" once the envoy is seated (P1 prompt: "small gilded folding screen"; P6 prompt: "small gilded screen").
3. **[HOUSE-C]'s marker vs glyph.** E03's house sign is a drop glyph, but its marker is the capped pen; E06's guest list names only the pen. Every other house's marker matches its glyph.
4. **E02's chair.** The Stair Envoy's marker sits on their own levelled chair, rolled into the row, not on one of the table's original chairs.
5. **E01's music block is older in form.** It has no Panel 2 cue line, does not list which chords mute the 543 pad (it says the pad is held only over chords with C# and fades out of any C natural), and states "no darksynth" only in the Panel 4 line. Consistent with 06, just less explicit than the later episodes.
