## 1. Premise

From 05 (B, The Full Hopper), verbatim:

> Vael Hollow, a dry commons, brings its last scraps to Recycler Bay, and the Tollkeepers bring their locked crates of spare parts. Each episode one CLEAN card becomes a small, real-in-story thing the Hollow builds for itself, until the Tollkeepers choose to open their crates.

Mercy-first, from 05: "The hoarders are invited, not raided. Nothing is taken by force. Abundance is earned and shared, never conjured."

From 05's Quest two, verbatim:

- In-story goal: "make enough for everyone without magic and without pretending."
- Setting: "Recycler Bay, plus invented places that come to it with a shortage, for example: Vael Hollow, a dry commons; the Tollkeepers, a guild that hoards spare parts."
- "Realistic means earned. Abundance on the page is built step by step from scraps, shared tools, and clean cards. Nothing appears from nowhere. Every gain costs a beat of patience (LET IT COOL)."
- "Hoarders are not punished. Their surplus is invited into the hopper, and the CLEAN card shows them a better deal."
- Never: "Real economic claims, real products, real prices, or promises about the real world."

Season shape: six standalone shorts (Q10, council ruling, delegated by Sherif 2026-10-01). Arc B's entry in 05 is three bullets long; it gives the premise, the modes and the mercy rule, but no per-episode beats (see open item 1).

## 9. Music plan (per `06_MUSIC_SPEC.md`)

Applies to all six episodes:

- **Tuning and cue.** Lab Night lead cue at A4 = 432 (06 §2): a cartoon-lab groove at 100–112 BPM, swung, with a loop that hiccups (tape-stop, stutter, skipped beat) and resumes. Three card stingers for OBVIOUS / FERAL / CLEAN: a plain chime, a growly detuned blip and a clean major bloom. Bubbly analog bass, square-wave blips, steam-noise sweeps, and the twelve-mask blip ring. Optional chiptune blips.
- **543 Hz top pad.** Held only over chords that contain C#, and muted over every other chord and any C natural.
- **528 Hz.** Only as the short chime on top of the fixed "That one can live." bloom, never held. "Blocked." is the fixed low, muted thud and filter close.
- **No darksynth.** There is no siege in this arc, so no 54 Hz drone either.
- **Wordless.** Every in-episode cue is wordless, including any choir swell (06 §5, Sung words). Sung words belong only to the series intro and outro themes.
- **Grounded.** Every episode ends on the Grounded motif: four notes rise and land back on the tonic, and hold.
- **Choir (ruled, council, #31 review).** One wordless open-vowel choir swell, in S3E06 only, as the crates open, mirroring S2E06: no lyric, no word-forming syllables, fading out before the 528 chime. No other S3 episode has a choir.
- **Drones (ruled, council, #31 review).** The Lab Night drone sits only under the 543-pad sections; the swung sections have no drone. Under those sections: 108 Hz (A2) for A-major and D-major beds, E2 80.91 Hz or B2 121.23 Hz for E-major beds, never A. The keys and drones are confirmed.
- **Names.** Mood words only, no artist, band, label or track names. The tuning is an aesthetic choice only, with no health claims (06 §4).
