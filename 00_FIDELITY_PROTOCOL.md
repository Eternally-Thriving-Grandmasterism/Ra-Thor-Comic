# 00 — Fidelity Protocol

Non-negotiable for every panel prompt.

1. **Canon armor.** Gold-and-black circuit plate. Winged halo ring. Narrow visor, warm eye-slit. Kite shield with an emerald eye-seal in circuit inlay. Thunder hammer grounded, not swung at people. Cape dark. Stance planted.
2. **Gate logic.** Ra-Thor blocks bullshit and passes awesome. The joke is the sorting, not the cruelty.
3. **Bubbles.** Short. One punchline per panel. No monologues in the bubble; strategy lives in the caption.
4. **Spice ceiling.** Suave Hour raise of an eyebrow (see `03_TONE_SPECS.md`), a line that could cut both ways. No explicit acts, no minors, no real people.
5. **No product theater.** Idea Recycler and White-Hat Mercy-Security are story machines. Captions may wink. They may not claim certification, affiliation, or legal authority.
6. **No likeness theft.** No celebrity faces. No xAI marks. Councils are silhouettes and seals, not portraits of living persons.
7. **Same Ra-Thor every episode.** If a prompt drifts the armor, reject the panel and rerun.
