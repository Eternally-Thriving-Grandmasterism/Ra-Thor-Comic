Set add-on for this episode: the Quiet Room with a long table and empty chairs down both sides, one practical lamp, deep shadow, spy-thriller elegance (Suave Hour mode), armor fully on. Winged-halo closed helm, warm gold visor slit, gold kite shield with the emerald eye-seal in circuit inlay, small hawk seal on the shoulder.
