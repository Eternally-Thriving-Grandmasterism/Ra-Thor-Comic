## Episode frame (CANON and ruled)

- **Mode and tempo:** Suave Hour, "Slow-burn, nocturnal synthwave at 88 BPM" (`episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L59). No borrowed bed (`seasons/S1_SEASON_SHEET.md` L61).
- **Tuning:** "Concert pitch A4 = 432 Hz" for all tonal material (`06_MUSIC_SPEC.md` L44). No stem stays at 440 (`06_MUSIC_SPEC.md` L46).
- **Key and tonic:** "A major bed over the 108 Hz drone." (`episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L60). "Tonic: A (A major bed, S1E01)." (`production/S1/S1E01/MUSIC_CUES.md` L32).
- **Lead (G7 B):** Ra-Thor's one lead synth timbre carries the answering lead in every cue with a melody (`screen/S1E01/GAP_RULINGS.md` L84). It is never pitch-bent upward at a phrase end (`06_MUSIC_SPEC.md` L22).
- **Bell (G6 A, G8 A):** the glassy bell sounds once in the whole episode, on the P2 lamp glint (`screen/S1E01/GAP_RULINGS.md` L74, L92). That is cue C02, shot S11.
- **SFX (G5 C):** room tone plus the three staged actions only: the scroll into the hopper, the cards landing in the tray, and the token set on the chair (`screen/S1E01/GAP_RULINGS.md` L66). No machine hiccup (`09_CHARACTER_BIBLE.md` L20).
- **Drone (G10 B):** the 108 Hz drone holds everywhere except the P5 gag. It drops for the gag and returns under the P6 stinger and Grounded (`screen/S1E01/GAP_RULINGS.md` L115).
- **No crawl cue** (G1–G3 A; `screen/S1E01/GAP_RULINGS.md` L30, L38, L46). No choir: the S1 choir swell is in S1E06 only (`seasons/S1_SEASON_SHEET.md` L66). Every cue is wordless (`06_MUSIC_SPEC.md` L112).

## Harmony and the 543 Hz pad (G9 B)

The G9 B ruling (`screen/S1E01/GAP_RULINGS.md` L102) sets these rules for every cue:

- **Plain D or E major chords are allowed, with the 543 Hz pad MUTED over them.** Plain D (D-F#-A) and plain E (E-G#-B) contain no C#, so the pad mutes or fades out over them (`06_MUSIC_SPEC.md` L64, "mute or fade the pad over chords without C#").
- **The pad holds only over C#-bearing chords:** "hold the 543 Hz top pad only over chords that contain C#" (`06_MUSIC_SPEC.md` L64). In this plan those are A, F#m, C#m, Dmaj7 and E6.
- **It is always muted over a C natural:** "always over a C natural" (`06_MUSIC_SPEC.md` L64) and "fades out of any chord with a C natural" (`episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L60). No cue in this plan uses a C natural.
- **Minor tinge in P1–P4:** F#m and C#m inside the A major bed (`episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L60; chords named in `06_MUSIC_SPEC.md` L64). This is the "colder, minor-tinged synthwave color" for an offer with strings (`06_MUSIC_SPEC.md` L36). No darksynth color: "there is no siege" (`episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L63).
- **Major blooms at P5 and P6:** the CLEAN card tone is "a clean major bloom" (`episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L64), and the P6 stinger is "an open major-chord bloom" (`episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L65).
- **528 Hz:** a short chime only, on top of the P6 bloom, never held (`06_MUSIC_SPEC.md` L63).

## Cue notes

- **C01 (cold open).** Enters under S01 and buttons out on the envoy's line at the end of CO2 (PROPOSED CANON — pending council ratification).
- **C02 (Act One).** The bell sounds once, on the lamp glint in S11, and nowhere else in the episode (`screen/S1E01/GAP_RULINGS.md` L74, L92).
- **C03 (stalemate).** This is where G9 B shows: plain D and E major enter, and the pad mutes over them (`06_MUSIC_SPEC.md` L64). The cue returns to C#m so the pad is back before the council.
- **C04 (council).** The pad holds through the council, as canon says (`episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L62), so the council chords stay C#-bearing. The cue settles on A under the silent visor beat in S20 (PROPOSED CANON — pending council ratification).
- **C06 and C09 (fixed stingers).** Both are "reused unchanged across episodes" (`06_MUSIC_SPEC.md` L23). This plan only places them.
- **C08 (recycler gag).** The only place the drone drops (`screen/S1E01/GAP_RULINGS.md` L115). It stays out through the TRADE WITHOUT KNOTS card in 4.2, which is still P5 (`episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L47).
- **C10 (Grounded).** "The episode ends there." (`episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L65). Nothing plays after it.
