## Arc summary

The Ribbon Envoy of [HOUSE-A] opens the Long Table with a gambit: a gift instead of terms (`episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L18). Accepted, the gift would bind every empty chair: "the scroll's ribbon trails gold threads to every empty chair" (`seasons/S1_SEASON_SHEET.md` L24).

Ra-Thor never raises the hammer. He works the problem in four strategic moves:
1. He names the gambit.
2. He consults the councils on how hard to block.
3. He blocks the offer but not the envoy.
4. He recycles the gift into a clean trade.

The envoy leaves with TRADE WITHOUT KNOTS and holds one chair with her token. The rest of the table stays open (`episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L52, L54).

The arc runs from courtesy under pressure to a fair deal kept in the open. The season premise works the same way: "One by one, Ra-Thor blocks the strings and keeps the deal" (`05_LORE_BIBLE.md` L160).

## Stakes

- **Canon stake:** the ribbon's threads reach every empty chair, and the block makes them "go slack and drop from the chairs" (`episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L40).
- **The strategic reading (PROPOSED CANON — pending council ratification):**
  - If Ra-Thor accepts, the first house's gift pre-binds every seat before any other house arrives. The table is lost before it is set.
  - If he refuses harshly, the season opens with a shamed house, which canon forbids: "No house is defeated or shamed." (`05_LORE_BIBLE.md` L162).
  - The narrow path is to block the offer and keep the envoy: "Ra-Thor blocks offers, not people." (`05_LORE_BIBLE.md` L122).
- **Mercy ceiling (CANON):** "Nobody is hit." (`episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L40). The hammer stays grounded throughout (`00_FIDELITY_PROTOCOL.md` L5, "Thunder hammer grounded, not swung at people.").

## How it seeds The Long Table

- **First chair spoken for (CANON).** The running tally starts here: "ONE CHAIR SPOKEN FOR. THE REST STILL OPEN. HAMMER STILL GROUNDED." (`seasons/S1_SEASON_SHEET.md` L39).
- **The marker chain (CANON).** The knot-glyph token starts the markers carried forward into later sets (`seasons/S1_SEASON_SHEET.md` L46, "Each single-envoy episode leaves its house's marker on a chair").
- **The rule of the table (CANON).** Every blocked envoy leaves with a CLEAN card (`05_LORE_BIBLE.md` L162, "Every blocked envoy leaves with a CLEAN card."). The CLEAN-card series begins with TRADE WITHOUT KNOTS (`seasons/S1_SEASON_SHEET.md` L33).
- **The open seats (PROPOSED CANON — pending council ratification).** The final image holds on the empty chairs as a promise. No total of chairs or houses is ever stated (`seasons/S1_SEASON_SHEET.md` L37, "no total of chairs or houses is ever stated").

## Cold open

### CO1. The table grows longer

- **Purpose:** establish the room, the empty seats and Ra-Thor's stillness before any word is spoken.
- **Move / counter-move:** none yet. The board is set.
- **Emotional turn:** quiet to expectant.
- **Anchor (CANON):** Set: "the Quiet Room, extended with a long table for the season." (`episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L7). The staging is Ra-Thor at the head, the shield leaned with the eye-seal out, and the hammer head-down with a small crack and sparks (`episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L19).
- **Anchor (PROPOSED CANON — pending council ratification):** a slow push down the empty chairs before the envoy is seen.
- **Sound (ruled):** room tone per G5 C (`screen/S1E01/GAP_RULINGS.md` L66). The cue enters "warm and unhurried" (`episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L61).

### CO2. The gambit is announced

- **Purpose:** the inciting line. A gift, not terms.
- **Move:** the envoy offers a gift with no stated terms. **Counter-move:** Ra-Thor does not answer yet.
- **Emotional turn:** courtesy that carries a hook.
- **Anchor (CANON):** title card "QUIET ROOM. THE TABLE GROWS LONGER. THE FIRST ENVOY ARRIVES." (`episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L17). The envoy says "My house sends gifts, not terms." (`episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L18).
- **Anchor (PROPOSED CANON — pending council ratification):** the cold open cuts out on her line, before main titles.

## Act One: the gift

### 1.1 The scroll on the table

- **Purpose:** show the gift and let its weight register.
- **Move:** the envoy keeps her gloved hands folded over the gilded scroll tied in gold ribbon (`episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L19). **Counter-move:** Ra-Thor reads it before he touches anything.
- **Emotional turn:** admiration edged with caution.
- **Anchor (PROPOSED CANON — pending council ratification):** a held two-shot down the table, in the Suave Hour grammar (`03_TONE_SPECS.md` L76, "Two-shots across a table").

### 1.2 Naming the gambit

- **Purpose:** Ra-Thor's first strategic move is to name what the gift really is.
- **Move:** Ra-Thor says "A gift with knots is a contract." (`episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L25). The speaker is Ra-Thor per G4 B (`screen/S1E01/GAP_RULINGS.md` L57). **Counter-move:** the veil gives nothing away.
- **Emotional turn:** charm becomes a negotiation.
- **Anchor (CANON):** title card "RIBBON COUNT: ONE. LOOSE ENDS: SEVERAL." (`episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L24). The shot is "Tight on the visor." (`episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L26).
- **Sound (ruled):** the glassy bell sounds once, on the P2 lamp glint (G6 A and G8 A; `screen/S1E01/GAP_RULINGS.md` L74, L92).

### 1.3 The envoy holds her ground (Act One out)

- **Purpose:** raise the pressure without raising voices.
- **Move:** the envoy does not withdraw the gift, so refusing it would read as a slight to her house. **Counter-move:** Ra-Thor stays still and lets the silence work.
- **Emotional turn:** polite stalemate.
- **Anchor (PROPOSED CANON — pending council ratification):** the whole beat. It follows the Suave Hour rhythm, where "The best line is usually the reply, not the opener." (`03_TONE_SPECS.md` L78).

## Act Two: how hard?

### 2.1 The councils convene

- **Purpose:** the deliberation, where the hard call is weighed in the open.
- **Move:** the twelve masks argue over how hard to block. **Counter-move:** one mask offers the shape of the answer: "Untie it. Keep her." (`episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L32).
- **Emotional turn:** tension becomes clarity.
- **Anchor (CANON):** title card "TWELVE MASKS. TOLC ON THE TABLE. HOW HARD?" (`episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L31). "Below them, Ra-Thor has not moved." (`episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L33). The masks advise and never outvote: "They do not outvote the Gate." (`09_CHARACTER_BIBLE.md` L28).
- **Sound (CANON):** "The drums drop out for the council." (`episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L62).

### 2.2 The choice is made, unseen

- **Purpose:** Ra-Thor decides before he acts. The audience sees the decision land on the visor.
- **Move:** Ra-Thor settles on blocking the knots and keeping the envoy. **Counter-move:** none yet; the envoy cannot read the visor.
- **Emotional turn:** resolve.
- **Anchor (PROPOSED CANON — pending council ratification):** a held visor beat with no line. It is grounded in 09's "Visor slit is the only expression" (`09_CHARACTER_BIBLE.md` L5).

## Act Three: the block

### 3.1 The threads reveal themselves

- **Purpose:** the reveal and midpoint turn. The gift's real reach becomes visible.
- **Move:** the envoy slides the scroll down the table, and its ribbon trails thin gold threads that reach every empty chair (`episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L40). **Counter-move:** the shield is already moving.
- **Emotional turn:** the stakes become visible. Every seat is at risk.
- **Anchor (CANON):** P4 staging, `episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L40.

### 3.2 Blocked

- **Purpose:** the strategic block, the episode's central action.
- **Move:** the shield comes down flat on the scroll. **Counter-move:** none possible; the threads go slack and drop from the chairs (`episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L40).
- **Emotional turn:** release, with no cruelty.
- **Anchor (CANON):** "Blocked." (`episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L39). Title card "THE OFFER IS BLOCKED. THE ENVOY IS NOT." (`episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L38). The shield's eye-seal must read clearly (`episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L42).
- **Sound (CANON):** the fixed stinger, a short, low, muted thud and a filter close (`episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L63).

### 3.3 The envoy, unharmed (Act Three out)

- **Purpose:** prove the block hit the offer, not the person.
- **Move:** the envoy's gloved hand lifts away, unharmed (`episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L40). **Counter-move:** she stays at the table, composed.
- **Emotional turn:** wary respect on both sides.
- **Anchor (PROPOSED CANON — pending council ratification):** she remains seated or standing and does not leave. That keeps "A blocked envoy is never humiliated" (`05_LORE_BIBLE.md` L122).

## Act Four: the clean trade

### 4.1 Recycle it

- **Purpose:** turn the blocked gift into something usable. The abundance turn of the Long Table.
- **Move:** "Recycle it." (`episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L46). The scroll goes into one of the twin hoppers, ribbon and all. **Counter-move:** the machine offers three paths.
- **Emotional turn:** wit and lightness.
- **Anchor (CANON):** the P5 staging. Three cards land, OBVIOUS, FERAL and CLEAN, and Ra-Thor reads only CLEAN (`episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L47; `09_CHARACTER_BIBLE.md` L20, "Ra-Thor reads only the third aloud.").
- **Sound (ruled):**
  - the scroll going into the hopper and the cards landing, per G5 C (`screen/S1E01/GAP_RULINGS.md` L66);
  - the drone drops for this gag only, per G10 B (`screen/S1E01/GAP_RULINGS.md` L115);
  - "Light and dry." (`episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L64).

### 4.2 TRADE WITHOUT KNOTS

- **Purpose:** the counter-offer. Ra-Thor gives back a better deal than the one that was blocked.
- **Move:** the CLEAN card says TRADE WITHOUT KNOTS (`episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L47). **Counter-move:** the envoy weighs it.
- **Emotional turn:** suspicion gives way to recognition.
- **Anchor (PROPOSED CANON — pending council ratification):** a held beat as the card crosses the table between them, before she takes it.

### 4.3 That one can live (Act Four out)

- **Purpose:** the deal is kept and a chair is spoken for.
- **Move:** the envoy takes the CLEAN card, veil still down, and gives the smallest bow. She sets her knot-glyph token on one empty chair (`episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L54). **Counter-move:** Ra-Thor answers "That one can live." (`episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L53).
- **Emotional turn:** wary respect becomes the first alliance.
- **Anchor (CANON):** P6 staging and bubble, `episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L53, L54.
- **Sound (CANON):** the fixed stinger, "an open major-chord bloom topped by the short 528 Hz chime" (`episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L65). The drone returns under it, per G10 B (`screen/S1E01/GAP_RULINGS.md` L115).
