## G9. Per-panel chords

- **Audit:** `production/S0_GAP_AUDIT.md` L132. **Source:** `production/S1/S1E01/MUSIC_CUES.md` L43. Inline markers: `production/S1/S1E01/MUSIC_CUES.md` L23, L24, L25, L26, L27, L28.
- **GAP text:** "Per-panel chords are not given, so where the 543 Hz pad sounds cannot be marked per panel." (`production/S1/S1E01/MUSIC_CUES.md` L43)
- **Option A — PROPOSED CANON — pending council ratification:** draw every panel's chords only from the C#-bearing set, so the 543 pad holds throughout. The set is A, F#m, C#m, Dmaj7 and E6 (`06_MUSIC_SPEC.md` L64, "A major, F# minor, C# minor").
- **Option B — PROPOSED CANON — pending council ratification:** mix C#-bearing chords with plain D or E major, muting the 543 pad over the plain chords and over any C natural (`episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L60, "fades out of any chord with a C natural"). Lean the P1–P4 strings section minor-tinged (`06_MUSIC_SPEC.md` L36, "A colder, minor-tinged synthwave color if the offer has strings").
- **Option C — PROPOSED CANON — pending council ratification:** keep as GAP until the score plan in Screen PR C (`screen/SCREEN_BIBLE_ADDENDUM.md` §3, M5).
- **Ruling: B (Hands/Clerk/Wild, 2026-10-02)**
  - Note: in 06 L36, "if the offer has strings" means strings attached to the offer (`06_MUSIC_SPEC.md` L36), as in "a gift that has strings" (`episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L3).
  - The same sense appears in "a deal that has strings" (`05_LORE_BIBLE.md` L160) and "offers something with strings" (`03_TONE_SPECS.md` L79).
  - Note: the minor tinge in P1–P4 comes from F#m and C#m inside the A major bed: "A major bed over the 108 Hz drone." (`episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L60). The chords are named in "A major, F# minor, C# minor" (`06_MUSIC_SPEC.md` L64).
  - Note: the harmony resolves to the major blooms at P5 and P6 (`episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L64, L65). The tonic is A: "Tonic: A (A major bed, S1E01)." (`production/S1/S1E01/MUSIC_CUES.md` L32).
  - Note: the 543 Hz pad holds only over C#-bearing chords and is muted over any C natural (`06_MUSIC_SPEC.md` L64, "always over a C natural"; `episodes/S1E01_THE_RIBBON_CLAUSE.md` L60, "fades out of any chord with a C natural").
