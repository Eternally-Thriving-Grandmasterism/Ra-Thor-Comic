## Arc summary

The Stair Envoy of [HOUSE-B] brings the Long Table its second gambit: "My house asks one small favor." (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L18). The favor is precedence: "a tall chair crested FIRST, so everyone else sits lower" (`seasons/S1_SEASON_SHEET.md` L25).

Ra-Thor never raises the hammer. He works the problem in four strategic moves:
1. He names the gambit.
2. He consults the councils on who sits where.
3. He blocks the precedence but keeps the trade.
4. He lets the guest recycle the crest into a level trade.

The envoy leaves with TRADE ROUTES. LEVEL CHAIRS. and sets their token on their own levelled chair. Two chairs are now spoken for (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L52, L54).

The arc runs from a polite bid for rank to a fair deal at equal height. The season premise works the same way: "One by one, Ra-Thor blocks the strings and keeps the deal" (`05_LORE_BIBLE.md` L160).

## Stakes

- **Canon stake:** the offer's fine print is rank at the table: "OFFER: TRADE ROUTES FOR THE FIRST SEAT. FINE PRINT: EVERYONE ELSE SITS LOWER." (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L24). The block keeps the value: "THE PRECEDENCE IS BLOCKED. THE TRADE ROUTES STAY ON THE TABLE." (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L38).
- **The strategic reading (PROPOSED CANON — pending council ratification):**
  - If Ra-Thor accepts, the second house sets a ranking before the other houses arrive, and every later chair starts lower.
  - If he refuses the trade with the chair, the season loses a fair deal and shames a house, which canon forbids: "No house is defeated or shamed." (`05_LORE_BIBLE.md` L162).
  - The narrow path is to block the rank and keep the routes: "Ra-Thor blocks offers, not people." (`05_LORE_BIBLE.md` L122).
- **Mercy ceiling (CANON):** "Nobody is hit." (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L40). The hammer stays grounded throughout (`00_FIDELITY_PROTOCOL.md` L5, "Thunder hammer grounded, not swung at people.").

## How it seeds The Long Table

- **Second chair spoken for (CANON).** The running tally continues: "TWO CHAIRS SPOKEN FOR. EVERY CHAIR THE SAME HEIGHT. HAMMER STILL GROUNDED." (`seasons/S1_SEASON_SHEET.md` L40).
- **The marker chain (CANON).** The step-glyph token joins the knot-glyph token from S1E01, which "still rests on one chair" (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L7). The chain runs on (`seasons/S1_SEASON_SHEET.md` L46, "Each single-envoy episode leaves its house's marker on a chair").
- **The rule of the table (CANON).** Every blocked envoy leaves with a CLEAN card (`05_LORE_BIBLE.md` L162, "Every blocked envoy leaves with a CLEAN card."). The second card is TRADE ROUTES. LEVEL CHAIRS. (`seasons/S1_SEASON_SHEET.md` L33).
- **Equal height (PROPOSED CANON — pending council ratification).** Every chair at the table now stands at one height, so no later house can buy a higher seat. The chairs stay uncounted: "Chairs run down both sides, uncounted." (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L7; `seasons/S1_SEASON_SHEET.md` L37, "no total of chairs or houses is ever stated").

## Act One: the tall chair

### 1.1 The chair at his right hand

- **Purpose:** bring the gambit into the room and let its height register.
- **Move:** "The tall chair now stands at Ra-Thor's right hand, its back towering over the whole row." (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L26). **Counter-move:** Ra-Thor looks at it before he looks at the envoy.
- **Emotional turn:** amusement edged with caution.
- **Anchor (PROPOSED CANON — pending council ratification):** the move from the tile to the room. Canon goes from the corridor on the tile (P1) to the chair at his right hand (P2); the arrival in between is new staging, shot over the shoulder on the offer (`03_TONE_SPECS.md` L76, "over-the-shoulder on the offer").

### 1.2 Naming the gambit

- **Purpose:** Ra-Thor's first strategic move is to name what the chair really asks for.
- **Move:** "The Stair Envoy rests a gloved hand on its gilded crest, which is engraved FIRST." (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L26). **Counter-move:** Ra-Thor says "Tall chairs make short talks." (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L25). The speaker is Ra-Thor per G4 B, on the narrowing visor (`screen/S1E02/GAP_RULINGS.md` L61; `episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L26, "Visor slit narrows.").
- **Emotional turn:** charm becomes a negotiation.
- **Anchor (CANON):** title card "OFFER: TRADE ROUTES FOR THE FIRST SEAT. FINE PRINT: EVERYONE ELSE SITS LOWER." (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L24).
- **Sound (CANON and ruled):** "Full groove. The lead answers the envoy's phrase with a short, dry figure." (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L62). The phrase answered is the envoy's P1 line, and the lead is Ra-Thor's timbre per G4 B and G6 B (`screen/S1E02/GAP_RULINGS.md` L61, L87).

### 1.3 The envoy holds their ground (Act One out)

- **Purpose:** raise the pressure without raising voices.
- **Move:** the envoy keeps their hand on the crest, so refusing the chair would read as refusing the routes. **Counter-move:** Ra-Thor stays still and lets the silence work.
- **Emotional turn:** polite stalemate.
- **Anchor (PROPOSED CANON — pending council ratification):** the whole beat. It follows the Suave Hour rhythm, where "The best line is usually the reply, not the opener." (`03_TONE_SPECS.md` L78).

## Act Two: who sits where?

### 2.1 The councils convene

- **Purpose:** the deliberation, where the hard call is weighed in the open.
- **Move:** the twelve masks argue over the seating. **Counter-move:** the council gives the shape of the answer: "Same table. Same height." (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L32). It is a council bubble; the staging places it at the mask at nine o'clock (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L33).
- **Emotional turn:** tension becomes clarity.
- **Anchor (CANON):** title card "TWELVE MASKS. ONE QUESTION: WHO SITS WHERE?" (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L31). "Two of them glance at the tall chair." (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L33). The masks advise and never outvote: "They do not outvote the Gate." (`09_CHARACTER_BIBLE.md` L28).
- **Sound (CANON):** "The drums drop out for the council." (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L63).

### 2.2 The choice is made, unseen

- **Purpose:** Ra-Thor decides before he acts. The audience sees the decision land on the visor.
- **Move:** Ra-Thor settles on blocking the rank and keeping the routes. **Counter-move:** none yet; the envoy cannot read the visor.
- **Emotional turn:** resolve.
- **Anchor (PROPOSED CANON — pending council ratification):** a held visor beat with no line. It is grounded in 09's "Visor slit is the only expression" (`09_CHARACTER_BIBLE.md` L5).

## Act Three: the block

### 3.1 Everyone else sits lower

- **Purpose:** the midpoint turn. The cost of the favor becomes visible down the row.
- **Move:** the chair's back towers over "a row of plain chairs" (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L27). **Counter-move:** the shield is already moving.
- **Emotional turn:** the stakes become visible. Every other seat is lower.
- **Anchor (PROPOSED CANON — pending council ratification):** a wide down the row that shows the plain chairs under the tall back before the shield moves. It stages the fine print already in the P2 caption (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L24).

### 3.2 Blocked

- **Purpose:** the strategic block, the episode's central action.
- **Move:** "Ra-Thor sets the shield gently against the tall chair's back." **Counter-move:** none possible; "The back folds down, section by section, until it is exactly as high as every other chair." (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L40).
- **Emotional turn:** release, with no cruelty.
- **Anchor (CANON):** "Blocked." (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L39). Title card "THE PRECEDENCE IS BLOCKED. THE TRADE ROUTES STAY ON THE TABLE." (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L38). The shield's eye-seal must read clearly (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L42).
- **Sound (CANON and ruled):** the fixed stinger, timed "to the chair back's last fold" (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L64); the fold itself per G5 C (`screen/S1E02/GAP_RULINGS.md` L76).

### 3.3 The envoy, unharmed (Act Three out)

- **Purpose:** prove the block hit the rank, not the person.
- **Move:** "The FIRST crest pops loose and lands on the table. Nobody is hit. The envoy steps back unharmed, shoulders shaking with a quiet laugh behind the mask." (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L40). **Counter-move:** "Hammer untouched, still grounded." (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L40).
- **Emotional turn:** the laugh turns the block into shared wit.
- **Anchor (PROPOSED CANON — pending council ratification):** the envoy stays in the room and does not leave. That keeps "A blocked envoy is never humiliated" (`05_LORE_BIBLE.md` L122).
- **Sound (CANON and ruled):** the crest landing per G5 C (`screen/S1E02/GAP_RULINGS.md` L76). "The groove resumes one bar later." (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L64).

## Act Four: the level trade

### 4.1 The guest feeds the machine

- **Purpose:** turn the blocked rank into something usable. The abundance turn of the Long Table.
- **Move:** "This time the guest feeds the machine. The Stair Envoy picks up the FIRST crest and drops it into one of the compact Idea Recycler's twin hoppers." (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L47). **Counter-move:** the machine offers three paths.
- **Emotional turn:** wit and lightness.
- **Anchor (CANON):** the P5 staging. Three cards land, OBVIOUS, FERAL and CLEAN, and "Ra-Thor reads only CLEAN aloud." (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L47; `09_CHARACTER_BIBLE.md` L20, "Ra-Thor reads only the third aloud.").
- **Sound (ruled and CANON):**
  - the crest going into the hopper and the cards landing in the tray, per G5 C (`screen/S1E02/GAP_RULINGS.md` L76);
  - the drone drops for this gag only, per G8 B (`screen/S1E02/GAP_RULINGS.md` L107);
  - "Light and dry." (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L65).

### 4.2 TRADE ROUTES. LEVEL CHAIRS.

- **Purpose:** the counter-offer. Ra-Thor gives back the trade without the rank.
- **Move:** Ra-Thor reads the CLEAN card aloud: "Trade routes. Level chairs." (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L46). The card says TRADE ROUTES. LEVEL CHAIRS. (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L47). **Counter-move:** [GAP — long look cut per X6; canon L54: envoy leaves with card in hand]
- **Emotional turn:** suspicion gives way to recognition.
- **Anchor (PROPOSED CANON — pending council ratification):** the card passes to the envoy here, once. Canon never stages the handover; it only has the envoy leave "CLEAN card in hand" (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L54).

### 4.3 That one can live (Act Four out)

- **Purpose:** the deal is kept and a second chair is spoken for.
- **Move:** "The Stair Envoy rolls the now-level chair into the row, sets a small step-glyph token on it, gives a short nod, and turns to go, mask still on, CLEAN card in hand." (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L54). **Counter-move:** Ra-Thor answers "That one can live." (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L53).
- **Emotional turn:** wary respect becomes the second alliance.
- **Anchor (CANON):** P6 staging and bubble, `episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L53, L54.
- **Sound (CANON and ruled):** the fixed stinger, "an open major-chord bloom topped by the short 528 Hz chime, never held." (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L66). The roll and the token per G5 C (`screen/S1E02/GAP_RULINGS.md` L76). The drone returns under the stinger, per G8 B (`screen/S1E02/GAP_RULINGS.md` L107).
