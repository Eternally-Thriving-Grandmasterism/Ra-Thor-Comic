## Episode frame (CANON and ruled)

- **Mode and tempo:** Suave Hour, "Slow-burn, nocturnal synthwave at 92 BPM (inside 84–96)." (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L59). No borrowed bed: "none in the music (P1 borrows a Quiet Board panel visually; the cue just starts sparse)" (`seasons/S1_SEASON_SHEET.md` L62).
- **Tuning:** "Concert pitch A4 = 432 Hz" for all tonal material (`06_MUSIC_SPEC.md` L44). No stem stays at 440 (`06_MUSIC_SPEC.md` L46).
- **Key and tonic:** "E-major bed, so the drone sits on E2 (80.91 Hz), never on A." (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L60). "Tonic: E (E-major bed, S1E02)." (`production/S1/S1E02/MUSIC_CUES.md` L32).
- **Lead (G6 B):** Ra-Thor's one lead synth timbre carries the answering lead in every cue with a melody (`screen/S1E02/GAP_RULINGS.md` L87). It is never pitch-bent upward at a phrase end (`06_MUSIC_SPEC.md` L22). The electric-piano keys stay the chord bed: "Smooth electric-piano-style chords" (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L59).
- **SFX (G5 C):** room tone plus the staged actions only: the P4 fold and the crest landing, the P5 hopper and tray, and the P6 roll and token (`screen/S1E02/GAP_RULINGS.md` L76). No machine hiccup (`09_CHARACTER_BIBLE.md` L20).
- **Drone (G8 B):** the E2 drone holds everywhere except the P5 gag. It drops for the gag and returns under the P6 stinger and Grounded (`screen/S1E02/GAP_RULINGS.md` L107).
- **No crawl cue** (G1–G3 A; `screen/S1E02/GAP_RULINGS.md` L30, L38, L46). No choir: the S1 choir swell is in S1E06 only (`seasons/S1_SEASON_SHEET.md` L66). Every cue is wordless (`06_MUSIC_SPEC.md` L112). No darksynth: "No darksynth anywhere; there is no siege." (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L59).

## Harmony and the 543 Hz pad (G7 B)

The G7 B ruling (`screen/S1E02/GAP_RULINGS.md` L97) sets these rules for every cue:

- **Mixed chords inside the E-major bed.** C#-bearing chords and chords without C# may both appear.
- **The pad holds only over C#-bearing chords:** "The 543 Hz top pad is held only over chords that contain C# (for example the A-major and F#-minor chords in the progression) and fades out everywhere else." (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L60).
- **It is muted over any chord without C#**, per the same line: "fades out everywhere else" (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L60).
- **No Dmaj7**, since D natural is not in E major (`screen/S1E02/GAP_RULINGS.md` L97).
- **Major blooms at P5 and P6:** the CLEAN card tone is "a clean major bloom" (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L65), and the P6 stinger is "an open major-chord bloom" (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L66).
- **528 Hz:** a short chime only, on top of the P6 bloom, never held (`06_MUSIC_SPEC.md` L63).
- **No progressions placed.** This plan names no chord sequence for any cue. Canon names only the A-major and F#-minor examples (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L60), so voicing is left to the composer under the rules above (PROPOSED CANON — pending council ratification).

## Cue notes

- **C01 (cold open).** Enters under S01 and buttons out on the envoy's line at the end of CO2 (PROPOSED CANON — pending council ratification).
- **C02 (Act One).** Enters with the full groove when the chair reaches the room. The lead answers the envoy's P1 phrase (`screen/S1E02/GAP_RULINGS.md` L61) after Ra-Thor's line in S11 (PROPOSED CANON — pending council ratification).
- **C03 (council).** The drums drop out, as canon says (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L63). The cue holds under the silent visor beat in S19 (PROPOSED CANON — pending council ratification).
- **C04 (the block).** The groove returns in 3.1 (PROPOSED CANON — pending council ratification). The fixed stinger lands on the last fold in S22, and the groove resumes one bar later (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L64).
- **C05 (recycler gag).** The only place the drone drops (`screen/S1E02/GAP_RULINGS.md` L107). It stays out through the TRADE ROUTES. LEVEL CHAIRS. card in 4.2, which is still P5 (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L47).
- **C06 (stinger and Grounded).** Both fixed stingers are "reused unchanged across episodes" (`06_MUSIC_SPEC.md` L23); this plan only places them. "The episode ends there." (`episodes/S1E02_THE_TALL_CHAIR.md` L66). Nothing plays after it.
