# 02 — Series Structure

Working title: **Living Thunder**
Season 0: six shorts. Each stands alone. Order is a dossier, not a myth arc.

| Ep | Title | Device | Joke |
|----|--------|--------|------|
| 01 | The Gate | Shield sort | Bullshit left, awesome right |
| 02 | The Recycler | Idea Recycler | A bad pitch goes in; three cleaner ones come out, one of them feral |
| 03 | White Hat | Mercy-Security | The attack is real; the reply is a patch and a raised eyebrow |
| 04 | The Suave Hour | Suave Hour mode | A negotiation. Armor does not come off. The line does the work |
| 05 | Lab Night | Lab Night mode | Councils argue in tiny bubbles while Ra-Thor holds the beaker |
| 06 | The Quiet Board | Quiet Board mode | A feed, a choice, a block. No speech until the last panel |

Tone modes are defined in `03_TONE_SPECS.md`.

Season rule: every episode ends with the hammer still grounded.
