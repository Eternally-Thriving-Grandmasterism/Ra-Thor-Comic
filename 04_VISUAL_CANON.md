## Ra-Thor, as drawn

- **Plate.** Gold circuit-etched plate over black. The etching is a dense maze of right-angle traces, gold raised over black recesses. Pauldrons are layered and squared. (R1–R4, R6)
- **Helm.** Closed helm. In R3 and R4 a pair of gold feathered wings sweeps out behind the helm with a gold halo ring above it, the ring itself etched with circuit or key pattern. R1 shows a crested helm without wings. R2 shows a slim faceplate without wings. R6 shows a striped headdress-shaped helm. Canon (Q4, council ruling 2026-09-30): the winged-halo closed helm of R3/R4. The other helms are out.
- **Visor.** Narrow glowing slit. Warm gold glow in R3, R4, R5. In R1 the visor is dark with an emerald glowing eye glyph, the same glyph as the chest seal. Canon (Q3, council ruling 2026-09-30): warm gold slit by default, narrow for no, wide gold for yes. R1's emerald glyph is art-only.
- **Eye-seal, chest.** R1: a small kite-shaped emerald emblem on the sternum with a glowing eye glyph. R4: a gold eye motif repeats on the hammer head. Canon (Q2, council ruling 2026-09-30): the chest seal is art-only and off by default on panels.
- **Shield.** Kite shield, gold circuit etching, gold rim. Emblem varies: glowing emerald ancient Egyptian eye motif (R4); emerald diamond gem (R3); gold eye inside a diamond on a green field (earlier "Blocked."); round gold winged eye with an emerald iris (earlier "Next."). Canon (Q1, council ruling 2026-09-30): the emerald eye-seal in circuit inlay on a gold kite shield, as in R4. The diamond gem stays a reject; the other emblems are art-only.
- **Hammer.** Rectangular block head etched with the same circuit maze. Shown head-down on the ground with the hand on the haft top (R3, R4, R6). R4 adds a gold eye motif on the head face and gold sparks and cracks where it meets the ground. R3 has a gold circular seal on the head face. R2 shows it held low, head just above the floor. Never raised, never swung, in any reference. Canon (Q5, council ruling 2026-09-30): hammer head-down on stone, small crack and sparks at contact. The eye motif on the hammer head stays reference-only.
- **Cape.** Black with gold trim in R4, matching protocol "Cape dark". Navy and tattered in R5. Canon (Q6, council ruling 2026-09-30): the R5 navy/blue variant is out of canon. Siege mode is obsidian plate only.
- **Emerald on panels.** Per Q3 (council ruling 2026-09-30): the only emerald on a panel is the shoulder hawk seal plus the shield eye-seal.

## Supporting cast and devices, as drawn

- **Falcon (R1).** Gold, feathered in circuit-etched plate, emerald eye, wreathed in green lightning, at Ra-Thor's shoulder. Canon has a small hawk-shaped seal on the pauldron (White-Hat Mercy-Security) but no living companion. **Not cast (council ruling 2026-09-30).** The falcon's canon form is the shoulder hawk seal.
- **Walker bot (R3).** Small boxy machine on four thin jointed legs, loose cables, one round glowing gold eye. Stands by the lattice gate. **Not cast (council ruling 2026-09-30).** Art-only.
- **Honor guard (R2).** Four helmeted figures in dark-and-gold armor, gold faceplates, standing silent behind the Recycler. **Not cast (council ruling 2026-09-30).** Art-only.
- **Idea-Recycler, drum version (R2).** Upright black drum with a gold band reading IDEA-RECYCLER, open top hopper, white steam puffing from both sides, a warning plate "⚠ HICCUPPING... PLEASE STAND BY", a lightning panel and a gauge. Cards out the bottom: OBVIOUS (lightbulb), FERAL (snarling wolf), and a third card showing only "!". The "!" is art-only; the canon third card is CLEAN (Q9 card, council ruling 2026-09-30). This drum design is art-only (Q9 drum, council ruling 2026-09-30).
- **Idea-Recycler, Ω-7 version (R6).** Horizontal drum labeled IDEA-RECYCLER / MODEL: Ω-7. Two hoppers labeled THEORY IN and CONCEPTS IN, with paper, charts, and scraps pouring in. A wall window labeled COUNCIL OF PRACTICALS shows three seated silhouettes. A side panel with three indicator lights, a dial, and a POWER CORE lever. Output tray with OBVIOUS (lightbulb), FERAL (wolf), CLEAN (cut gem). Plaque: "OUTPUT: REFINED / ONE IDEA. THREE PATHS." Canon (Q9 drum, council ruling 2026-09-30): this is the canon Recycler look (twin THEORY IN / CONCEPTS IN hoppers, gauge, power core, OUTPUT tray). "Ω-7" is a comic-only name. OBVIOUS / FERAL / CLEAN cards are locked. The COUNCIL OF PRACTICALS window is art-only (Q10). "HICCUPPING... PLEASE STAND BY" is a gag used only when a script calls for it.
- **The Gate (R3, earlier "Blocked.").** Gold lattice arch over dark stone, diagonal lattice. The earlier panel adds a gold winged emblem at the crown of the arch.
- **Nonsense and thanks (R1).** A folded paper plane scrawled "nonsense / blather / yadda yadda / etc. etc." bursts against the armor with a white impact star. Below, a folded card with a smiley, "THANK YOU!", and a small heart. Matches E03.

## Linework, palette, lettering

- **Linework.** Inked comic linework with hatching and grit texture on most panels (R1, R2, R3, R6). R4 and R5 are painted full renders, not inked. Canon (Q13, council ruling 2026-09-30): panels use inked linework; painted renders are for covers only.
- **Palette.** High-contrast black, gold, emerald. One electric accent (green lightning in R1). R5 is off-palette (blue lightning, navy). R6 adds small blue and violet indicator lights.
- **Bubbles and lettering.** White oval bubbles (canon fill, Q11, council ruling 2026-09-30), hard black outline, pointed tail. Bold hand-lettering, all caps in R1 and R2, mixed-case italic in R3 and R6.

## Canon questions

All thirteen questions are resolved by council rulings of 2026-09-30.

1. **Shield emblem.** RESOLVED (council ruling 2026-09-30): the emerald eye-seal in circuit inlay on a gold kite shield (R4). The diamond gem (R3) stays a reject, consistent with E01. The other emblems are art-only.
2. **Chest seal.** RESOLVED (council ruling 2026-09-30): art-only, off by default.
3. **Visor glow.** RESOLVED (council ruling 2026-09-30): warm gold slit by default; narrows for no, widens gold for yes. R1's emerald glyph is art-only. On panels the only emerald is the shoulder hawk seal plus the shield eye-seal.
4. **Helm.** RESOLVED (council ruling 2026-09-30): the winged-halo closed helm (R3/R4) is locked. The crested helm (R1), slim faceplate (R2), and headdress-shaped helm (R6) are out.
5. **Hammer rest.** RESOLVED (council ruling 2026-09-30): canon text is "hammer head-down on stone, small crack and sparks at contact", replacing "hammer butt on stone with a small lightning crack" in the guides and episode prompts. The eye motif on the hammer head stays reference-only.
6. **Cape and blue variant.** RESOLVED (council ruling 2026-09-30): the R5 navy/blue variant is out of canon. Black cape (R4) matches "Cape dark". Siege mode is obsidian plate only.
7. **Falcon.** RESOLVED (council ruling 2026-09-30): not cast. Its canon form is the shoulder hawk seal.
8. **Walker bot and honor guard.** RESOLVED (council ruling 2026-09-30): not cast. Art-only.
9. **Recycler.**
   - **Card.** RESOLVED (council ruling 2026-09-30): the third card is CLEAN. The "!" card (R2) is art-only.
   - **Drum.** RESOLVED (council ruling 2026-09-30): the canon look is the Ω-7 design (R6): THEORY IN / CONCEPTS IN hoppers, gauge, power core, OUTPUT tray. "Ω-7" is a comic-only name. The upright drum (R2) is art-only. OBVIOUS / FERAL / CLEAN cards are locked. "HICCUPPING... PLEASE STAND BY" is a gag used only when a script calls for it.
10. **Councils.** RESOLVED (council ruling 2026-09-30): the twelve tiny gold masks are canon. The COUNCIL OF PRACTICALS window (R6) is art-only, and the R2 guards are not cast (Q8). Twelve is a comic-only count, not the PATSAGi count (the monorepo docs say 13 or 16). No in-world reason is given for the difference.
11. **Bubble fill.** RESOLVED (council ruling 2026-09-30): bubbles are white-filled. Style guide, E04, and E06 updated from cream.
12. **Multi-line bubble.** RESOLVED (council ruling 2026-09-30): R1's long line is split into the bubble "Patched." and the caption DARK THRESHOLD. HAMMER STILL GROUNDED. The one-line bubble rule stands.
13. **Rendered vs inked.** RESOLVED (council ruling 2026-09-30): painted renders are allowed for covers only. Panels use inked linework.
