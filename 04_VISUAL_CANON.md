## Ra-Thor, as drawn

- **Plate.** Gold circuit-etched plate over black. The etching is a dense maze of right-angle traces, gold raised over black recesses. Pauldrons are layered and squared. (R1–R4, R6)
- **Helm.** Closed helm. In R3 and R4 a pair of gold feathered wings sweeps out behind the helm with a gold halo ring above it, the ring itself etched with circuit or key pattern. R1 shows a crested helm without wings. R2 shows a slim faceplate without wings. R6 shows a striped headdress-shaped helm. Canon (Q4, amended ISSUE01-CANON-TIDY-1): the winged closed helm, with the Eye of Horus in the halo sigil. The other helms are out.
- **Visor.** Narrow glowing slit. Warm gold glow in R3, R4, R5. In R1 the visor is dark with an emerald glowing eye glyph, the same glyph as the chest seal. Canon (Q3, amended ISSUE01-CANON-TIDY-1): two glowing white-hot gold eyes behind the T-visor, narrow for no, wide gold for yes. R1's emerald glyph is art-only.
- **Eye-seal, chest.** R1: a small kite-shaped emerald emblem on the sternum with a glowing eye glyph. R4: a gold eye motif repeats on the hammer head. Canon (Q2, amended ISSUE01-CANON-TIDY-1): a plain gold sun disc with radiating rays and no bolt. The 2026-09-30 "no chest seal" line is amended (Sherif, 2026-10-14, 10:07 PM). R1's chest emblem stays art-only.
- **Shield.** Reference images vary: glowing emerald ancient Egyptian eye motif (R4); emerald diamond gem (R3); gold eye inside a diamond on a green field (earlier "Blocked."); round gold winged eye with an emerald iris (earlier "Next."). Canon (Q1, amended ISSUE01-CANON-TIDY-1): the tall riveted obsidian and antique-gold shield with a central Vegvísir and decorative gold runes. No Eye of Horus on the shield. The 2026-09-30 line, an emerald eye-seal on a gold kite shield, is amended (Sherif, 2026-10-16, 2:15 AM and 2:16 AM ET). The diamond gem stays a reject; the other emblems are art-only.
- **Hammer.** Rectangular block head etched with the same circuit maze. Shown head-down on the ground with the hand on the haft top (R3, R4, R6). R4 adds a gold eye motif on the head face and gold sparks and cracks where it meets the ground. R3 has a gold circular seal on the head face. R2 shows it held low, head just above the floor. Never raised, never swung, in any reference. Canon (Q5, amended ISSUE01-CANON-TIDY-1): a remix of the mythological Mjölnir (Norse myth), block head with lightning bolts on the faces, cone-bottom hilt, no eye. Plain rectangular block head (a squared box head), one large clear lightning bolt on at least both faces, a straight handle and a simple cone pommel. No Eye of Horus on the hammer. Based on the myth only, never Marvel's design (no leather-wrapped handle with a wrist strap, no copying Marvel's head detailing, no Marvel names). Any on-page or store-metadata use of the name Mjölnir needs a trademark check before publishing. Optional decoration: small Norse runes and ancient Egyptian hieroglyph-style marks as Easter eggs, engraved on the head sides or handle, decorative only, no readable words, no real code, no names, nothing coined, never covering or replacing the bolt on each face (triangle top retired 2026-10-16, Sherif). Head-down on stone, small crack and sparks at contact, unless a beat says raised; never swung at people. Hammer eye rejected.
- **Cape.** Black with gold trim in R4, matching protocol "Cape dark". Navy and tattered in R5. Canon (Q6, council ruling 2026-09-30): the R5 navy/blue variant is out of canon. Siege mode is obsidian plate only.
- **Buckle.** A rose-gold lightning bolt (Sherif, 2026-10-14, 10:07 PM). Not a winged sun-disc buckle. Not a winged scarab buckle.
- **Emerald on panels.** Per Q3 (amended ISSUE01-CANON-TIDY-1): emerald appears only on the pauldron seal, the winged Eye of Horus in emerald gemstone green (Sherif, 2026-10-16, 4:08 AM ET). The 2026-09-30 shield eye-seal is amended. No Eye of Horus on the shield.

## Linework, palette, lettering

- **Linework.** Inked comic linework with hatching and grit texture on most panels (R1, R2, R3, R6). R4 and R5 are painted full renders, not inked. Canon (Q13, council ruling 2026-09-30): panels use inked linework; painted renders are for covers only.
- **Palette.** High-contrast black, gold, emerald. One electric accent (green lightning in R1). R5 is off-palette (blue lightning, navy). R6 adds small blue and violet indicator lights.
- **Bubbles and lettering.** White oval bubbles (canon fill, Q11, council ruling 2026-09-30), hard black outline, pointed tail. Bold hand-lettering, all caps in R1 and R2, mixed-case italic in R3 and R6.

## TOLC visuals (council ruling 2026-10-15; new art only)

Wild's ruling for the councils (2026-10-15, 9:58 PM ET; lore in `05_LORE_BIBLE.md`, TOLC visuals). New art only: the reference images above and finished Issue 1 art are not redrawn. This section sits at the end of the file so that existing line citations into 04 do not move. Refined 2026-10-15 10:52 PM ET (Sherif and council ruling): V3's inner ring is TOLC8, and V4's points of light are the mercy checks.

- **V3, concentric rings (adopted).** On the lattice arch, a fixed inner ring, with fainter outer growth around it. Do not draw a set count of outer rings. The fixed inner ring is TOLC8; the twelve council masks sit on it (10:52 PM ET).
- **V4, the Mercy-Ways as paths of light (adopted; refined 2026-10-15 10:52 PM ET).** The thin, unlabelled gold paths through the lattice are the Mercy-Ways. Small, uncounted points of light along them are the mercy checks. When something is weighed at the TOLC and passes, the paths run past the LIVE lane and converge on V3's fixed inner ring (TOLC8), where the twelve council masks sit. Never draw a countable set of paths or points.
- **V5, geometry as texture (adopted).** Faint convergence linework (orbits, curves settling to a centre, a converging spiral) etched into the arch stone, as texture only.
- **Prompt add-on (new art at the TOLC):** "gold lattice arch with a fixed inner ring and fainter outer growth, twelve tiny gold council masks on the inner ring, thin unlabelled gold paths of light running past the LIVE lane and converging on the inner ring, small scattered points of light along the paths, too many and too faint to count, faint converging-orbit linework etched into the arch stone as texture, no numbers, no text, no equations".
- **Reject:** V1 (a ring of countable light points) and V2 (an eight-sided keystone), because both are numbering nods. Any number, label, plaque or icon for the mercy checks or the Mercy-Ways; a countable row or list of checks. Equations or written symbols on the arch. TOLC mathematics anywhere on the page; any expansion or gloss of TOLC8 or PATSAGi.
- **Held:** V6 (a single source light above or behind the arch). Not drawn unless the councils rule on it.
